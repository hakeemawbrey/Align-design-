You don't need a sign. You need to admit you already got one.

You keep asking for clarity. A message, a dream, a number on a clock.

But be honest. The sign came. It was the knot in your stomach. The relief when the plan fell through. The friend who said it gently and you changed the subject.

We ask for signs when we already know and are hoping for a different answer.

The universe is not being vague. You are being polite to something you have already outgrown.
