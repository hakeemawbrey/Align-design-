Sagittarius, the best trips have a second ticket. Buy it.

The archer loves freedom. New cities, open roads, the next big thing.

Sometimes they treat love as something that would slow them down. They pack light, leave early and tell themselves travelling alone is the whole point.

But the best adventures are shared. The sunset means more when someone you love is standing next to you, laughing at the same moment.

Sagittarius, you do not have to choose between freedom and love. The best trips have a second ticket. Buy it for someone who wants to wander with you.
