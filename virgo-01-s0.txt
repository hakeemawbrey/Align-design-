Virgo shows love by fixing things. Let them. It's their version of a poem.

Virgo is mutable earth, ruled by Mercury. Practical, detailed, devoted.

They may not write you a love letter. But they will fix your shelf. Book the appointment you kept forgetting. Bring the plaster before you knew you needed it.

That is their romance. Care in action.

If you are with a Virgo, notice the small things they do. Every one of them is a sentence in a very long poem.
