Capricorn ages backwards. Serious at twenty, silly at forty. Stick around.

There is an old astrology saying that Capricorns age in reverse. Saturn rules them, and Saturn rewards time.

They are often old souls when young. Responsible early. Worried about the future. They were the kid who worried about the bills and planned the family holiday.

Then, as the work pays off, they loosen up. The humour comes out. They finally relax.

If you meet a young Capricorn, be patient. The best version is often still on its way.
