Capricorn doesn't say I love you until they've built something to put it in.

Capricorn is cardinal earth, ruled by Saturn. Structure, responsibility, long game.

They do not rush declarations. Words are commitments, and Capricorn takes commitments seriously. Saturn rewards patience, and Capricorn courts the way they climb mountains, one secure step at a time.

Before they say it, they want a foundation. Trust. Plans. Proof that this is real.

When a Capricorn finally tells you they love you, believe it. It was not said lightly. It was built.
