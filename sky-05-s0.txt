Compatibility isn't matching signs. It's two charts that know how to share a room.

People love the shortcut. Fire with air, earth with water, done.

Real synastry is richer. It is how your moon meets their Venus. Where your Mars rubs against their Saturn. Which parts of you light each other up and which parts clash.

Every pairing has friction. The question is whether you both know how to live with it.

The best matches are not perfect fits. They are good roommates in the same sky.
