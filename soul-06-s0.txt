Not everyone who drains you is a villain. Some are just not your people.

It is tempting to make it a story. They are toxic. They are selfish. They are energy vampires.

Sometimes that is true. Often it is simpler. You are just different. Different pace, different values, different idea of fun.

You do not need a villain to leave a room. You can just notice how you feel there and choose another.

Let people be who they are, somewhere else. That is kinder for everyone, including you.
