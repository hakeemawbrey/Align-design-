Under the Aries bravado is someone who just wants to be picked first.

Aries looks like the most confident person in the room. Loud laugh, quick opinions, first through the door.

But under the armour is often a simple wish. To be chosen. Not as an afterthought. Not as the backup. First. Under the speed is someone who has often been told they were too much, and kept running anyway.

That is why they push so hard. They are making sure you notice.

Pick them first, out loud, once in a while. You will see the warmth under the fire.
