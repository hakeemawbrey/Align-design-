Nobody you admire skipped this part. They just tell it faster now.

It does not feel meaningful right now. It feels heavy. Confusing. Slow.

But every version of you that you admire got built in a season like this. The one who left. The one who started again. The one who stopped waiting.

The hard part is not a detour. It is the road.

Keep walking. One day you will tell this story, and you will be glad you did not quit in the middle.
