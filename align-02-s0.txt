Forcing it is how you end up with the wrong thing that fits badly.

You can make almost anything work if you push hard enough. The wrong job. The wrong flat. The wrong person.

It will fit the way a borrowed coat fits. Too tight in the shoulders. Never quite comfortable. You will spend years adjusting it and calling the discomfort commitment.

Alignment does not need force. It needs honesty about what actually fits.

If it takes everything you have to keep it together, ask whether it was ever meant to be together.

Stop forcing it. Come align. Link in bio.
