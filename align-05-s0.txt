You don't find alignment. You stop doing the things that block it.

We picture alignment as a destination. Something out there, waiting to be found.

More often, it is what is left when you stop. Stop saying yes when you mean no. Stop staying where you are tolerated. Stop shrinking to keep the peace. Stop reaching for what you already know is not yours, just because it is nearby.

The right things are already trying to reach you. The clutter is in the way.

Clear a little, and watch what flows in.

Stop forcing it. Come align. Link in bio.
