A Cancer goes quiet instead of saying it hurt. Ask. They're waiting for you to ask.

The crab retreats into its shell. That is how Cancer protects itself.

When they are hurt, they rarely say so directly. They get quieter. A little distant. They hope you will notice. They are hoping you care enough to look closer without being asked.

It is not a game. It is fear of being too much.

If your Cancer has gone quiet, do not wait it out. Ask, gently. You will be amazed how quickly they come back out.
