Capricorn isn't cold. They're just protecting the thing they took years to build.

Capricorn can seem guarded. Slow to warm. Careful about who they let in.

That is because they have built a life with effort. Every brick earned. Late nights, careful savings, years of quiet work nobody clapped for.

They are not going to let just anyone walk in and rearrange it.

Patience and consistency win a Capricorn. Show them you value what they have built. Then offer to help build more.
