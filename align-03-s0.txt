The right person at the wrong time is still the wrong person.

We love the story. Two people who were perfect for each other, if only.

But the timing is part of the person. Their readiness. Their choices. What they prioritise right now. Wanting you is not the same as being ready to choose you, and only one of those builds anything.

If they cannot meet you where you are, they are not right for you yet, maybe not ever.

That is not tragic. It is clarifying. Alignment needs the person, the place and the moment to agree.
