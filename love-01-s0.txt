You don't miss them. You miss who you were allowed to be around them.

It is rarely the person. It is the version of you that showed up when they were there.

Maybe you were funnier with them. Softer. Less careful. Maybe it was the only time you let yourself want something out loud. When it ended, you lost access to that version of you, and it felt like losing them.

Here is the good news. That person was never theirs. They were yours the whole time. They just needed permission.

So stop waiting for someone to hand it back. Be that person on a Tuesday, alone, for no reason. Then notice who shows up for it.
