Peace is not boring. You're just used to living on adrenaline.

If your life has been loud for a long time, calm can feel like something is missing.

No crisis to solve. No person to manage. No drama to replay. Just a quiet evening, and an odd restlessness in your chest.

That restlessness is withdrawal, not wisdom. Your system is used to stimulation, and it is asking for more.

Stay with the quiet anyway. Let it get comfortable. Peace is an acquired taste, and it is the best one you will ever acquire.
