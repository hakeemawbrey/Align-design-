Sagittarius laughs through almost everything. Notice when they stop.

Sagittarius is the optimist of the zodiac. They find the joke in the disaster. They keep the mood up.

That is lovely, and it can hide a lot. The laugh is real, but sometimes it is also a very good way of changing the subject.

If your Sagittarius goes quiet, if the jokes stop, something is wrong. They are not good at saying so directly.

Sit with them. Ask. The archer needs someone who knows that laughter is not always the whole story.
