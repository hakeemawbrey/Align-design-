### Season 14: Cuffing Season (24)
The temperature drops and all twelve signs suddenly want someone to share a blanket with. Each sign tries to "cuff" in
its own way, and THE STARS keep steering them toward the real thing: being honest about what they want.
1. The first cold night · 2. Aries asks in October energy · 3. Taurus builds the blanket fort first, finds the person later ·
4. Gemini's three almost-dates · 5. Cancer has the soup ready · 6. Leo wants a winter photo partner ·
7. Virgo's cuffing spreadsheet · 8. Libra can't pick between two scarves (and two people) · 9. Scorpio, all in or nothing ·
10. Sag says "I'm not looking", is looking · 11. Capricorn schedules a feelings meeting · 12. Aquarius invents a heated bench ·

### Season 15: Home for the Holidays (24)
The signs go home: bringing a date to the family table, the cousin who asks "so are you seeing anyone?", the gift
exchange, the long drive back. Warm and funny, never mean about families.
Episode beats to write: one per sign bringing someone home (12), plus 12 shared moments: the seating chart, the
"so what do you do?" uncle, the kids' table, the photo, the gift you forgot, the game that got too competitive, cooking
together, the quiet walk after dinner, the call to a friend who stayed in town, leaving early kindly, the drive home
debrief, and the finale "Where we're going next year".

### The Thread (32) — new sitcom
The twelve signs share one group chat. Each episode is a single thread: someone posts, everyone reacts in character,
one sign needs advice, and the chat (usually Virgo) has it. Running gags: Pisces sends crying voice notes, Leo pins its
own message, Capricorn sets an agenda, Gemini replies to three messages at once. Arcs across the month: Libra's
holiday party RSVP, Sag's surprise trip, the secret gift exchange, and Cancer organising the year-end dinner.

### Star Talk Game Night (24) — new game show
THE STARS host. Two signs per episode, three quick rounds ("Finish the text", "Green flag or red flag", "What would
you do?"), and the winner is whoever answers most kindly, not fastest. 12 episodes pair opposite signs and 12 pair
signs of the same element.

### 24 Days of Green Flags (24)
An advent calendar: a door opens each day of December up to the 24th, and behind it is one small green flag
("remembers how you take your tea", "texts when they get home", "says sorry without a 'but'"). Short, warm, made to
be shared. Post each one first thing so the calendar is easy to follow.

### Sag Season Takeover (12) and Capricorn Season Takeover (8)
Sagittarius runs the account until about 21 Dec: trips, big ideas, honest-to-a-fault moments. Then Capricorn takes
the keys: plans, goals, quiet loyalty and a surprisingly soft side. The handover episode is the 21st.
