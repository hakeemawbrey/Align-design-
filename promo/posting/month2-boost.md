## What's different in this batch

1. **No card transition and no end card at all.** No Align logo, wordmark, "come Align" animation, CTA or card-back
   cut anywhere, and no tarot border. The video ends on its last beat: either a hard cut on the punchline or a
   **seamless loop** back to frame 0, so it replays on its own. Every format below says which.
2. **Crazier hooks.** Frame 0 is already mid-chaos: something mid-crash, mid-zoom or mid-scream, plus a big hook line
   of at most 7 words. Then:
   - **First second:** a pattern break, such as a whip zoom, a record scratch, sudden silence or a colour flash.
   - **By second 3:** the relatable premise has landed.
   - **Shorter lengths:** star_talk 10–25 s, advice posts 15–30 s, trial reels 6–12 s.
3. **Super relatable.** Every video names a feeling people actually have, such as:
   - left on read, the situationship, being single at the dinner, missing an ex;
   - the 2 a.m. scroll, being the friend who gives advice and never takes it.

   The joke is on the situation, never on a sign.
4. **The house rules still apply:**
   - Pisces cries in every video.
   - No real brands, apps, platforms, songs or people.
   - No invented stats.
   - No disaster, weapon or explosion jokes, nothing sexual, no drinking jokes.
   - Advice is warm and never controlling.

   App features are optional props only (talk cards, tonight's deck); there's never a logo or pitch.

### Visual hook kit (shared, so 48 sessions all hit the same bar)
A small shared module of reusable hook effects that every film can use:
- **Motion:** whip zoom in, record scratch with a freeze frame, a slam-in title, screen shake, a "caught" red circle.
- **On screen:** a full-screen fake incoming call, a phone notification drop, a loading bar ("overthinking… 99%"),
  a countdown, a news ticker, a video-game HUD.
- **Endings:** a loop-back helper that matches the last frame to the first.

It's built first, and every session imports it.

## How it fits the schedule

- **Times:** new videos slot in halfway between the existing ones on the same account, so each stream posts every 45
  minutes. star_talk still starts at 8:00 AM, and star_advice posts still start at 8:45 AM, with trial reels after
  them. Exact times go in the final plan.
- **Mix:** no two videos from the same format back to back on an account; the existing day plan stays untouched.
- **Seasonal touches:** light December flavour (cold weather, the holidays, New Year's resolutions) where it fits,
  but most videos are evergreen, so anything left over still works in January.
- **Deliverables:** the same as before. Every video goes to Google Drive (a new `Month 2 boost` folder), and the
  posting plan is rebuilt as one combined schedule of 1,800 videos with captions and Drive links.

## How we make it

- **Kit first:** one session builds the shared hook kit (about an hour), then 48 build sessions start, each with
  12–24 videos.
- **Same pipeline:** the same process that just delivered the 900, with three changes: no end card, no card cut and
  no border.
- **Rough timing:** the last batch took about 5 hours from launch to all 900 uploaded. These videos are shorter, so
  expect about the same or a little faster, plus the Drive copy and the schedule rebuild.
