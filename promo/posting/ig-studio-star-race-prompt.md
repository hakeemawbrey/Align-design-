Update the posting plans to add a new series: **The Star Race**, a 32-video marble-run (plinko-style) story where the 12 zodiac signs race down a cosmic marble run. I've made three versions of every video, one for each use below. All files are in Google Drive and every link is listed below with its caption. Captions are final: no hashtags, no emoji.

## What goes where

| Account | Version | Drive folder | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|
| **star_talk** (star_talk.md) | A: the originals | Star Talk / The Star Race (marble run) | **20 posts a day** |
| **star_advice** (star_advice.md), posts | B: flash-forward opening, new music, navy frame | Star Talk / The Star Race - Account B | **10 posts a day** |
| **star_advice** (star_advice.md), trial reels | C: hook on the first frame, "Previously" or slow-mo replay, new music | Star Talk / The Star Race - Trial reels (C) | **10 trial reels a day** |

## Rules

1. **Start tomorrow morning** (Thu 26 Nov) at each account's first slot of the day.
2. **Star Race videos take the first slots of each day** on their account, in the order listed below, until the series is used up. Keep the existing slot times and the spacing between star_talk and star_advice so the two accounts never post at the same minute.
3. **Don't drop anything already planned.** Videos pushed out of those slots move later in the plan, in their original order, and the plan's end date moves out to fit them.
4. **Keep the story order exactly as listed:** Race 1 → the 12 sign POVs of Race 1 → the 12 between-race episodes → the 6 "night before Race 2" episodes → Race 2 last (it's the finale).
5. **The three versions never cross accounts:** A only on star_talk, B only as star_advice posts, C only as star_advice trial reels. Within an account no video repeats.
6. **On star_advice, post B and its matching C on the same day**, with the trial reel in a different slot from the post.
7. Use each video's caption exactly as written below.

That's 32 videos per version, so the series runs:
- **star_talk:** 20 on Thu 26 Nov, then 12 on Fri 27 Nov (Race 2 lands Friday).
- **star_advice posts:** 10 a day Thu 26 to Sat 28 Nov, then 2 on Sun 29 Nov.
- **star_advice trial reels:** the same split as the posts.

On days the series doesn't fill, the remaining slots go back to the existing plan.

When you're done, show me the new Day 1 and Day 2 schedules for both accounts and tell me the new end date of each plan.
