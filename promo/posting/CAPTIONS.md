# Caption guide — Star Talk short videos (zodiac + dating, for a zodiac audience)

Each job entry is one video. Use your own film: the on-screen lines, title card
and THE STARS' advice are in there. Write the caption from what the video actually shows. Never describe things that
aren't in the film.

## Voice per account (`for` / `alt_for`)
- **star_talk post** — the entertainment account. Funny, quick, a little knowing. The joke of the video lands in the
  caption from a new angle (don't just repeat the title). Often ends with a light question to the comments
  ("Which sign is your group chat's Virgo?").
- **star_advice post** — the advice account. Warm, kind, grounded. Name the feeling, then the healthy takeaway from the
  film in plain words (one or two sentences). Can end with a gentle prompt ("Save this for your next first date.").
- **star_advice trial** — a trial reel shown mostly to people who don't follow yet. The FIRST line must be a hook that
  works on its own for a stranger (a relatable moment or a question about their sign). Then one line of payoff.
  Short.
- Moonlight Lane (has `episode`): start with "Moonlight Lane, Ep N:" and tease the story without spoiling the ending,
  then the episode's relationship takeaway.

## Hard rules
- NO hashtags. No "#" anywhere. No emoji.
- 1–3 short sentences, max ~220 characters (trial reels max ~160). Use line breaks sparingly (at most one).
- No real brands, apps, platforms, songs, celebrities, or real people's names. Don't name the posting app.
  Don't mention "Align". No links, no "link in bio".
- No invented stats or facts ("80% of Scorpios…" is banned). Astrology talk stays playful, never presented as science.
- Advice is warm and healthy: honesty, boundaries, self-respect, kindness. Never suggest controlling, testing, tracking,
  jealousy games or punishing a partner. No shaming any sign.
- Nothing sexual, no weapons, no explosions, no disaster or flood jokes, no drinking-to-cope.
- Every caption is different. Don't reuse a hook or closing line across captions in your batch.
- If a video has `alt_for`, also write `alt`: a fully different caption (new hook, new angle) in that account's voice.
  It is the same video posted later on the other account.
