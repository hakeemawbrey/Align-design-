## Style (same as dummies v2)
- **Sky and grain:** the nebula sky (`drawSky(ctx, t, SKY_H)` from sky.js, with SKY_H from promo/films/wheel.js) and grain at GRAIN_H. One crescent moon at top-right. Open with a fade up from black over about 0.4 s. Close with a fade to black over the last 0.7 s; the last frame is black.
- **Type:** headlines in **Inter 800** (`TYPE.head`, about 108 px; it can go to 140–170 px for a short hook or end line), bone #efe6d6, with ONE accent word in **EB Garamond italic** in a colour. Punchlines in **Inter 700**, about 62 px (`TYPE.sub`). Optional Space Mono 700 eyebrow. Keep side margins of at least 70 px. Headlines sit near the top (block within y 160–560) and punchlines low (y 1480–1780).
- **Motion:** springy and playful. Headlines pop in word by word with overshoot (`easeOutBack`), things bounce, squash and settle, and little pixel-star sparkle bursts fire when something lands. Nothing strobes.
- **Illustrations:** bold, friendly line art with thick glowing strokes (5–9 px) and flat fills at 20–40%, in the Align palette (sign, element and gold colours). No faces on anything, and people are simple pictogram silhouettes. **Give every beat its own visual.** The zodiac wheel (`drawWheelH` etc. from wheel.js) is allowed, but it may fill at most about 25% of a film's screen time, and only where it genuinely helps.
- **Colours** (from promo/films/wheel.js `SIGNS`, `ELEMENT_COLOR`): fire #f0603a, earth #e9b24a, air #7fd0f0, water #3f7cf0. Sign colours as in SIGNS. Gold #f2c75c.
- **No ad:** no Align lockup or logo, no app screens, no product pitch. End on a fun, bold end line with a small flourish, then the fade.
- **Readability:**
  - 15 s films: headlines fully readable for at least 1.4 s and punchlines for at least 1.2 s.
  - Longer films: at least 1.8 s for headlines and 1.5 s for punchlines.
- **The hook lands in the first second.**
- **Sound:** cues in your scenes' CUES:
  - `['pop', t, freq, gain≤0.07, pan]` for every pop-in; vary freq 600–1400 to make a melody.
  - `['pluck', t, f, gain, decay, pan]`, `tick`, `whoosh`, `bell`, `glitter`, `riser`, and `boom` (sparingly).
  - You own your film's cues.js (108 bpm template; a bar is 2.222 s, beats at 0.6 + n·0.5556). Land key hits on beats.
