# Star Talk, lens A: hyper-relatable dating moments

The stars are your funniest friend: dramatic, petty, judgy but loving, and they can see everything from up there. Each spot opens on a moment people already screenshot for the group chat. The stars react the way your best friend would, then land one healthy piece of advice and one real Align feature.

Format notes for every concept:
- Lines are typed into the RPG text box, at most about 50 characters each.
- `[ ]` marks a visual or sound beat.
- Stars only act with their eyes and bodies (no mouths), so every reaction beat is written as an eye or body move.
- No frame just before the end card says "Align". The end card itself reads "come Align".

---

## 1. "We're up." (30 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** a phone lock screen fills the frame in the night sky. Notification: **"2:07 AM · u up?"**

**Beats:**
- **0–2 s:** `[phone buzzes, lone chiptune ping]` The notification hangs there.
- **2–5 s:** `[hundreds of tiny stars snap awake at once, all eyes wide, all staring at the phone]`
  - Box, THE STARS: **"we're up. we're stars. we're always up."**
- **5–9 s:** `[the gold narrator star drifts close to the phone and squints]`
  - THE STARS: **"and we've noticed he only texts after dark."**
- **9–13 s:** `[a big pixel crescent Moon slides in from the edge, eyes darting away, awkward]`
  - ☾ THE MOON: **"don't drag me into this. I'm just lighting."**
- **13–19 s:** `[the stars turn back to you, slow blink in unison]`
  - THE STARS: **"someone who only shows up at night?"**
  - `[beat]` **"babe. that's OUR job. not his."**
- **19–24 s:** `[the phone dims, the stars soften to happy eyes; tonight's deck deals in, card backs fanning out]`
  - THE STARS: **"want something new every night?"**
  - **"get a deck that deals every night."**
- **24–27 s:** `[one card glows; the stars bob]`

**Last line:** **"Find someone who texts in *daylight*."**

**Relatable truth:** everyone has had (or been) the 2am "u up". The person who only appears after midnight is not planning a future with you.

**Align tie-in:** tonight's deck, dealt nightly. The one nightly ping worth waiting for.

## 2. "A star just went out." (15 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** headline: **"“so… what do you do for *work*?”"** `[a sky full of stars, every eye drooping]`

**Beats:**
- **0–3 s:** `[stars' eyelids sink one by one, a little snore blip each]`
  - Box, THE STARS: **"oh no. not the job question."**
- **3–5 s:** Box, YOUR DATE (just a name tag, no one shown): **"any siblings?"**
  - `[one star in the corner flickers, its eyes close, and it goes out: tiny pixel poof, sad descending chiptune]`
- **5–9 s:** `[every other star snaps wide-eyed and turns slowly to camera]`
  - THE STARS: **"a star just went out. that's on you two."**
  - **"it burned for billions of years."**
  - **"it could not survive 'any siblings?'"**
- **9–12 s:** `[a talk card flips in over the sky, e.g. "What's a hill you'd die on?"; the dead star pops back on with sparkle eyes]`
  - THE STARS: **"see? ask something real. we live."**
- **12–15 s:** `[the whole sky brightens]`

**Last line:** **"Save a star. Ask something *real*."**

**Relatable truth:** first-date small talk is a job interview nobody applied for. Everyone has died a little inside at "so what do you do?"

**Align tie-in:** talk cards instead of small talk.

## 3. "k." (15 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** a single giant **"k."** centred on black. `[ominous low chiptune sting]`

**Beats:**
- **0–2 s:** `[pull back: three stars huddled round the "k." like a crime scene]`
- **2–4 s:** ♍ VIRGO STAR (squinting, tiny pixel magnifying glass):
  - **"lowercase. a PERIOD. no emoji."**
- **4–6 s:** ♊ GEMINI STAR (bouncing, eyes darting both ways):
  - **"is it k or K or kk? bc kk is fine"**
- **6–8 s:** ♋ CANCER STAR (eyes welling, one pixel tear):
  - **"they hate me. it's over. I'm moving."**
- **8–11 s:** `[the gold narrator star drifts in, half-lidded and deadpan, and scrolls the chat up one message. Your text above the "k.": "ok see you at 7!"]`
  - THE STARS: **"...you said 'ok see you at 7'."**
  - **"they said 'k'. that's the whole conversation."**
- **11–13 s:** `[all three freeze; Virgo slowly lowers the magnifying glass]`
  - THE STARS: **"Mercury isn't retrograde. this one's on you."**
  - `[a talk card flips in]` **"less decoding. more talking."**

**Last line:** **"Sometimes k just means *okay*."**

**Relatable truth:** spiralling over a one-letter text that, in context, meant exactly what it said.

**Align tie-in:** talk cards. Real questions mean there's less to decode.

*Astrology check:* Mercury rules both Gemini and Virgo, the two most likely to over-analyse a text, and it's what everyone blames for miscommunication when it's retrograde.

## 4. "That's archaeology." (15 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** a notification slams in: **"❤ liked your photo from 2019"**

**Beats:**
- **0–3 s:** `[a row of stars zoom in on the notification, eyes huge]`
  - Box, THE STARS: **"no text. no 'hey'. a like. from 2019."**
- **3–6 s:** `[the ♑ Capricorn star puts on a tiny pixel explorer hat and dusts the phone with a little brush]`
  - ♑ CAPRICORN STAR: **"that's not interest. that's archaeology."**
- **6–9 s:** `[a trail of pixel breadcrumbs runs across the sky, each one a label: "viewed your story", "🔥", "we should hang sometime". The stars hop along it like pigeons.]`
  - THE STARS: **"a story view. one 🔥. a 'we should hang'."**
  - **"babe. those are crumbs."**
- **9–13 s:** `[Capricorn takes off the hat, eyes firm. Tonight's deck deals in as a full fanned hand.]`
  - THE STARS: **"you deserve a full deck. every night."**

**Last line:** **"Stop living on *crumbs*."**

**Relatable truth:** breadcrumbing. Just enough attention to keep you hoping, never enough to make a plan.

**Align tie-in:** tonight's deck, dealt nightly. A whole fresh hand instead of scraps.

*Astrology check:* Capricorn is ruled by Saturn, the planet of standards, structure and commitment, so it's the sign most likely to refuse partial effort.

## 5. "Supernova." (30 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** headline: **"Day 3: “I think you're my *soulmate*.”"** `[the ♈ Aries star bounces so hard it squashes, heart-eyes]`

**Beats:**
- **0–5 s:** `[pixel text bubbles pop around Aries: "good morning beautiful 🥺", "told my mom about you", "matching outfits friday??". Two tiny matching pixel hoodies drop onto Aries.]`
  - ♈ ARIES STAR: **"THIS IS SO FAST. I LOVE FAST."**
- **5–10 s:** `[the gold narrator star drifts in with a slow, knowing blink]`
  - THE STARS: **"sweetie. we're stars. we know burning."**
  - **"the ones that burn this bright, this fast..."**
- **10–13 s:** `[a huge star behind them swells, flashes white and pops into a supernova; pixel debris drifts past; Aries goes wide-eyed]`
  - THE STARS: **"...don't stick around."**
- **13–16 s:** `[Aries peeks out from inside the hoodie]`
  - ARIES: **"...but the matching hoodies tho"**
  - `[every star in the sky rolls its eyes in sync, chiptune "bwomp"]`
- **16–21 s:** `[the big golden Sun rolls into frame and gives a slow, proud wink]`
  - THE STARS: **"ask the Sun. 4.6 billion years. slow burn."**
  - **"if it's real, it'll still be there on date 2."**
- **21–26 s:** `[two charts appear side by side, and the Push / Pull / Align reading draws between them]`
  - THE STARS: **"see the real pull before the fireworks."**
- **26–28 s:** `[Aries takes off one hoodie and folds it neatly]`

**Last line:** **"Slow burns *last*."**

**Relatable truth:** love-bombing. "Soulmate" on day 3 feels amazing and should worry you, and everyone's friend has said "this is moving kind of fast" in the group chat.

**Align tie-in:** the Push / Pull / Align compatibility reading. Real chemistry you can see, instead of a fireworks show.

*Astrology and science check:* Aries is ruled by Mars, so it's impulsive and loves speed. Massive stars really do burn hotter and die younger, often as supernovae. The Sun is about 4.6 billion years old.

## 6. "Even stars get a name." (30 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** headline: **"“so… what are we?”"** `[every star in the sky leans in, eyes wide; heartbeat chiptune]`

**Beats:**
- **0–4 s:** `[the suspense holds, and one tiny star covers its eyes with its points]`
- **4–7 s:** Box, THEM (name tag only): **"idk. I don't really do labels."**
  - `[every star's eyes go flat, all at once]`
- **7–11 s:** `[the ♒ Aquarius star pipes up from the back, chill and breezy]`
  - ♒ AQUARIUS STAR: **"labels are kind of a construct tbh"**
  - `[every star turns slowly to stare at Aquarius; it shrinks a few pixels]`
- **11–17 s:** `[the stars snap into a constellation, gold lines connecting them, and a little name plate pops up underneath]`
  - THE STARS: **"we are balls of gas. and WE have a name."**
  - **"we've had it for thousands of years."**
- **17–21 s:** AQUARIUS: **"...can't we just vibe?"**
  - THE STARS: **"you can vibe AND have a name."**
- **21–26 s:** `[a dealbreakers card slides in and a chip lights up: "Looking for: something real"]`
  - THE STARS: **"say what you want before the first date."**
- **26–28 s:** `[Aquarius sighs and slots into the constellation; the name plate glows]`

**Last line:** **"Even stars get a *name*."**

**Relatable truth:** the "what are we" talk, the "I don't do labels" dodge, and the situationship that never gets a name.

**Align tie-in:** dealbreakers up front. You've both said what you want before anyone has to ask.

*Astrology check:* Aquarius, a fixed air sign, is the sign everyone jokes is detached and allergic to labels. The zodiac constellations really have carried names for thousands of years, since Babylonian astronomy.

## 7. "Neptune is literally fog." (30 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** headline: **"Every group chat has *this* friend."** `[the ♓ Pisces star zooms in, heart-eyes at full blast]`

**Beats:**
- **0–4 s:** ♓ PISCES STAR: **"GUYS. I met someone. he's different."**
  - `[a row of friend stars (♍ Virgo, ♏ Scorpio, ♑ Capricorn), all half-lidded]`
- **4–8 s:** VIRGO STAR: **"is he a DJ."**
  - PISCES: **"he's a sound *artist*."**
- **8–12 s:** SCORPIO STAR: **"does he 'not believe in labels'?"**
  - PISCES: **"he's healing."**
- **12–15 s:** CAPRICORN STAR: **"has he asked to borrow money."**
  - PISCES: **"...only a little."**
  - `[all three roll their eyes in perfect sync, chiptune "bwomp"]`
- **15–20 s:** `[the gold narrator star floats in, gentle]`
  - THE STARS: **"babe. Neptune rules you."**
  - **"it is literally the planet of fog."**
  - `[pixel fog rolls in, and Pisces' heart-eyes turn into rose-tinted heart glasses]`
- **20–24 s:** `[a friend star nudges the glasses off: far behind, a tiny red flag is waving]`
  - PISCES: **"...is that a red flag?"**
  - THE STARS: **"it's been waving since june."**
- **24–28 s:** `[a dealbreakers list pops up and Pisces taps chips: "✕ borrows money", "✕ allergic to plans"]`
  - THE STARS: **"pick your dealbreakers BEFORE the heart-eyes."**

**Last line:** **"Love is blind. Your friends *aren't*."**

**Relatable truth:** everyone has the friend who always falls for the wrong one, and the group chat saw every red flag months ago.

**Align tie-in:** dealbreakers up front. You choose your non-negotiables while your head is clear.

*Astrology check:* Neptune, Pisces' modern ruler, is the planet of dreams, illusion and fog. That's why Pisces gets the rose-tinted-glasses jokes.

## 8. "Is it, though?" (15 s)

**Hook (0–1 s):** headline: **"He clapped when the plane *landed*."** `[the ♎ Libra star's heart-eyes cut instantly to flat]`

**Beats:**
- **0–3 s:** `[Libra's tiny pixel scales tip hard to one side]`
  - ♎ LIBRA STAR: **"it's over. I have the ick."**
- **3–6 s:** `[the gold narrator star, deadpan]`
  - THE STARS: **"babe. you clap at the end of movies."**
- **6–8 s:** `[Libra freezes, eyes wide, pixel sweat drop]`
  - LIBRA: **"...that's different."**
  - THE STARS: `[slow blink]` **"is it, though?"**
- **8–12 s:** `[a mystery card flips in, face hidden: "Card first. Face last."]`
  - THE STARS: **"everyone's a little cringe. look for kind."**
- **12–15 s:** `[Libra's scales level out and its eyes go soft]`

**Last line:** **"Give the ick a *minute*."**

**Relatable truth:** the ick. One tiny harmless thing ends it, while we're all doing equally cringe things ourselves.

**Align tie-in:** "Card first. Face last." You get to know who someone is before the little things take over.

*Astrology check:* Libra is ruled by Venus (beauty, harmony, aesthetics), which makes it the sign most famous for catching the ick.

## Top 3

1. **"We're up." (#1):** "babe. that's OUR job. not his." is the series premise turned into a punchline: of course the stars are up at 2am, and only they get to show up at night.
2. **"That's archaeology." (#4):** "liked your photo from 2019" is painfully specific, and "that's not interest, that's archaeology" is a caption people will screenshot and send to the friend it's happening to.
3. **"k." (#3):** the scroll-up reveal ("you said 'ok see you at 7'") is a real surprise twist in 15 seconds, and the Mercury line flips the go-to excuse back on the viewer.
