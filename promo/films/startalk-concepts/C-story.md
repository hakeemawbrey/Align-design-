# Star Talk, season 2: the sky is a little too invested in your love life

**Lens:** story and emotion with a comedic twist, in formats with a built-in payoff.
The first five were *advice* ("Dear Scorpio, do this"). Advice is a lecture, and nobody shares a lecture.
These are **mini-stories**: the stars have feelings, history and opinions about *you*, and every spot has a setup, a turn and a payoff you'd send to a friend.

## The recurring cast (a light bible, so these run as episodes)
- **YOUR STAR**: the viewer's sun-sign star. It is earnest and a bit needy, and it has loved you since the minute you were born, because it's literally in your chart. Every spot that uses it can ship **12 sign cuts**: a new tint, badge and name tag, with the same script. Each concept notes its default cut.
- **♋ AUNTIE CANCER** (Moon-ruled, the zodiac's mom) and **♉ AUNTIE TAURUS** (Venus-ruled, lives for a good dinner). They wear tiny pixel reading glasses and are overinvested.
- **THE BOOTH**: **♊ GEMINI** (Mercury-ruled, talks fast) and **♐ SAGITTARIUS** (Jupiter-ruled, blunt). They're opposite signs who share one mic, in pixel headsets.
- **♍ VIRGO**: the director and planner, with a beret and a clipboard.
- **♓ PISCES**: always in the background, always in tears (happy ones).
- **THE STARS**: the bone/gold chorus and narrator.

Text-box lines are at most ~50 characters. A name tag sits on every box. None of the frames just before the end card say "Align".

## Astrology fact-check (everything above is standard Western astrology)
- **Rulers:** Venus rules Taurus and Libra; Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo; the Moon rules Cancer; Mars rules Aries; Jupiter rules Sagittarius; Saturn rules Capricorn.
- **Elements:** Leo and Sagittarius are both fire, and same-element signs form a trine. Gemini and Sagittarius are opposite signs.
- **5th house:** romance, dating and pleasure.
- **Saturn return:** Saturn's orbit is about 29.5 years, so the first return lands around ages 27–30.
- **Mercury retrograde:** Mercury appears to move backwards a few times a year for about three weeks, and it's traditionally linked to communication mix-ups.
- **No statistics are claimed anywhere.** The only numbers are story details (2:14 AM, "Ep. 4", "Take 47", 9PM).

## Ranking: top 3

1. **#6 Our Stars Met First.** It has the strongest built-in payoff of the eight: the awkward-parents setup, the "YOU'RE FIRE??" turn and the "we don't have lips" button. The button is a joke only *this* character design can make, and it sells "Card first. Face last." as a sweet secret rather than a feature.
2. **#3 Live from Your 5th House.** Everyone has lived the "so… what do you do" silence. The reality-TV booth makes it a laugh, and the hidden-answer talk card gives a real suspense-and-release beat that ends in genuine joy.
3. **#1 The Star Who Won't Give Up.** The most emotional of the eight: it's funny (a shooting star bouncing off your head), but it lands on being loved by something that keeps showing up for you. It also restates the whole premise of the series ("astrology is talking to the stars, and they talk back").

## Which could become a recurring series
**"Live from Your 5th House"** is the franchise. THE BOOTH (Gemini and Sagittarius, opposites sharing one mic) can call any dating moment as a new episode: the first text, the reveal, meeting the friends, the second date, the "what are we" talk. Each episode cuts to a different real feature, with Pisces crying in the back as the running gag. Open each episode with the **"PREVIOUSLY ON…"** cold open from #2 to recap the last cliffhanger in 2 s.

There's a strong second lane in **#6 Our Stars Met First**, run as a sign-pairing series: Leo × Sagittarius (fire trine), Taurus × Scorpio (opposites: "you're… very intense." / "you're very… still."), Gemini × Virgo (both Mercury-ruled). It's twelve signs' worth of meet-cutes, and every one ends on "We don't have lips."
