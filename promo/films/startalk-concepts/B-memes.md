# Star Talk, round 2: zodiac meme culture (lens B)

The idea: astrology Twitter and TikTok already have the jokes. Every sign has a bit everyone knows: Taurus and food, Capricorn and work, Libra who can't decide, Sagittarius at the airport. These concepts hand those bits to the pixel stars and let them roast *you* (and each other) the way your group chat does.

Each concept makes fun of a dating situation the viewer has actually been in: the dry "hey", the fish pic, the third date with the same type of person again. The sign jokes are the delivery, not the premise.

Format notes for every concept:
- **Name tags** look like "♉ TAURUS STAR". "THE STARS" is the plain gold narrator star.
- **Text-box lines** are at most about 50 characters.
- **Fast cuts:** in the rapid-fire concepts, each star gets about 1.5–2 s on screen. That means a short line, a held reaction and a hard cut.
- **No "Align"** in the frames just before the end card. The end card reads "come Align".

## Top 3

1. **#2, The 12 stars rate your dating profile.** The fish pic is the most universally recognised dating-app joke there is, so it lands even for people who don't know their sign. And "Card first. Face last." pays it off perfectly: the photo was never the point.
2. **#1, "hey".** The dry "hey" is something everyone has received and sent. The rapid-fire sign reactions are a ready-made "tag yourself" list, and talk cards are the obvious answer to it.
3. **#7, ✨her date tonight✨.** It's a mirror of every real friend group before a date, so it gets shared to group chats as "this is literally us". The "wait, what does he look like?" turn sells the blind-card feature through a punchline rather than an explanation.
