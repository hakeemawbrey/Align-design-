# b44 · Hot Take, Cold Take (winter edition) · episodes 1–24 (nhce01–nhce24)

star_advice trial reels, 14 s each (story 0–11.6, then the short 2.4 s end card; changed 4 Nov from 16 s with the 4.4 s card). Bug: **HOT TAKE, COLD TAKE**.
Shared look: `promo/films/nhce01/scenes/hckit.js` (imported by nhce02–24).

**The format.** One winter dating moment, two extremes, one healthy middle.
- **0–2.4 hook (frame 0):** the moment as a bold top caption; the screen is split down the middle, the left half on
  fire (embers, warm glow, a HOT TAKE tag) and the right half frozen (snow, frost, a COLD TAKE tag). The hot sign is
  mid-hop on the left, the cold sign shivers on the right, a pixel thermometer stands between them. Works with the
  sound off for someone who has never seen the account.
- **2.4–5.3 HOT TAKE:** white-flash jump cut, the fire half takes the whole screen, the thermometer shoots up. Header
  "HOT TAKE", the take on a sticker, the hot sign acts it out with a prop and one bubble line.
- **5.3–8.2 COLD TAKE:** an icy white-flash jump cut (no card-back cut since 4 Nov), the frozen half takes the screen, the thermometer drops. Same layout.
- **8.2–11.6 WARM TAKE:** the halves melt into one amber frame, the thermometer settles on JUST RIGHT, both signs
  calm down, THE STARS say the last line in the dialogue box. Then the end card.
- Pisces cries in every film: in the corner with tissues through the takes, happy-tear fountains in the warm take
  (when Pisces is one of the two signs, it cries as itself). Every sign plays hot twice and cold twice, so nobody is
  typecast; the joke is the extreme, never the sign.
