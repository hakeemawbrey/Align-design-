# b16 · Remix: Snow Globe Cities, episodes 1–14 (star_talk post, 30 s, page 15)

Every film: the city is a snow globe on screen at t = 0 with the hero sign's star inside it mid-hop, the hook headline
up at frame 0, the bug "REMIX: SNOW GLOBE CITIES" top-left, and a Pisces star pressed against the glass from outside,
crying (sniffle → streams → fountains). Story 0–25.6 s, then the 4.4 s end card (default CTA). Beats: hook + two box
lines (0–6.4) · rule of three, third one twisted, and a reaction line (6.4–12.7) · card cut 12.9 · the turn: someone
new in the globe and THE STARS' advice (13.0–20.0) · the globe shakes, the snow swirls, the tie-in lands and the last
line pops (20.2–25.6). Shared look: promo/films/nsge01/globekit.js.

Cities taken by eps 1–14 (episodes 15–28 should pick others): Minneapolis, Montreal, Boston, Denver, Philadelphia,
Pittsburgh, Anchorage, Vancouver, Milwaukee, Ottawa, Calgary, Winnipeg, Quebec City, Salt Lake City. The first
Remix set used Houston, NYC, LA, Chicago, Austin, Miami, Atlanta, Nashville, Seattle, Dallas, New Orleans, Toronto.
