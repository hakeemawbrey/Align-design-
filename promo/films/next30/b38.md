# Next 30, batch b38: Your Sign at the Holiday Party (npye01–12) · The Signs as Winter Gifts (ngie01–12)

Both series are star_advice trial reels, 14 s each (story 0–11.6, then the 2.4 s short end card; changed from 16 s on 4 Nov, see NEXT30-BUILD.md). One sign per episode,
Aries → Pisces. Shared look in `promo/films/npye01/scenes/kit.js`.

**Format (both):** frame 0 = the headline already on screen ("*Aries* at the | holiday party:" or "If *Aries* were a |
winter gift:") with the sign star mid-action. Beat 1 (2.4–5.2) and beat 2 (5.2–8.0, the turn, under the one card cut)
each get a numbered caption sticker. Finale 8.0–11.6: THE STARS give the advice (the last line), the hero does the
hook action again, changed. Pisces cries in the background of every beat (happy tears at the finale); in the Pisces
episodes Pisces is the hero and cries throughout. Bug: the series name, top-left. Advice is warm, never controlling.

**Party set:** a cozy room: plum wall, a window on the night sky with falling snow, string lights, a garland, guests.
**Gifts set:** a snowy night under a pixel pine; a wrapped box in the sign's colour; Pisces is the happy recipient.
