Series: **Remix: Snow Globe Cities** · star_talk · post · 28 s each (story 25.6 s + the 2.4 s short end card, default CTA; CHANGE 4 Nov).

outside the glass and talk to them; **Pisces presses their face to the glass and cries** in every film (in ep 26 Pisces
is the lead and cries inside). Bug: "REMIX: SNOW GLOBE CITIES", top-left. Shared look: `promo/films/nsge15/globekit.js`.
Every film: frame 0 = the hook headline + the globe mid-shake with the lead mid-action; no card cut (CHANGE 4 Nov), only the
13–14 s; the tie-in is light, in the last beat. Cities never used by the first Remix set (no NYC, LA, Chicago, Austin,
Miami, Atlanta, Nashville, Seattle, Dallas, New Orleans, Houston or Toronto) and none repeated in this batch.
