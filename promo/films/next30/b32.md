  fight bubbles volley; Pisces sniffles behind a tissue box.

Lengths include the 4.4 s end card (default CTA). Two signs per episode; every sign except Pisces appears at least twice.

Arc for eps 13–24: b31's eps 1–12 cover the heat of it (the pause, curious questions, always/never, saying it back,
one fight at a time, or→and, taking and keeping a sorry, what's under it, off the screen, humour timing) and end on
"plan the next one calm". Eps 13–24 are the next layer: when to talk, whose side you're on, tone, comfort before fixes,
privacy (not in public, not in the group chat), owning your part, letting small things tie, the morning-after check-in,
the kind guess, letting a mended thing rest, and a year-end finale: thank them for staying in the hard talks.
