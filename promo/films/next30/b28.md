# b28 — Star Talk Explains, Volume 2, episodes 16–30 (star_advice posts)

The classroom format from Volume 1 (STARTALK16.md) carries on: frame 0 is THE QUESTION with the student mid-"huh?",
the teacher builds it on the chalkboard, every claim gets its tag (✓ REAL ASTRONOMY · ✦ IN ASTROLOGY · ♥ RELATIONSHIP
TIP), the student's lightbulb, THE SHORT VERSION, the last line, the default end card (4.4 s, included in the length).
Series bug "STAR TALK EXPLAINS" top-left. No season numbers anywhere. Pisces cries in every film: as the teacher or
student, or else from a little desk at the back of the class (happy tears at the lightbulb).

The engine is `nx2e16/scenes/x2kit.js` (a copy of the Volume 1 classkit, plus the bug, one card cut, the crying Pisces
and the "season board"); every other film imports it.

**Arc of 16–30.** Episodes 16–27: "What does each sign's season feel like?", one per sign, starting with the season we're
in (Sagittarius) and going round the year. The season board is the same every time: a 12-slot zodiac strip that the Sun
slides along into this sign's slot, the dates, then ELEMENT · MODE · RULER, then three chalk words for the "feel".
Episodes 28–30: the houses for family and friends (3rd: siblings and neighbours; 4th: home and family; 11th: friends
and hopes), ending the volume on 28 Dec with friendship.

**Facts used (and only these).** Season dates are given as "about" (the Sun's entry moves by a day between years).
Cardinal seasons start at an equinox or solstice; the December solstice is the longest night and the June solstice the
longest day in the Northern Hemisphere; at the equinoxes day and night are close to equal. Brightest stars: Regulus
(Leo), Spica (Virgo), Antares, a red supergiant (Scorpius); Castor and Pollux are Gemini's two brightest stars, named
for mythological twins. Element, mode and ruler are standard Western astrology (traditional and modern rulers named
where they differ). The scales are the only zodiac symbol that is an object. Houses are counted from the rising sign;
in many house systems the 4th starts at the IC, the bottom of the chart. All "feel" readings are framed as astrology.
