# b30 — Holiday Survival Guide, episodes 13–24 (star_advice posts, 30 s)

Episodes air one a day from Sun 6 Dec, so 13–24 land from about 18 Dec into the last week of the year: the arc runs
from the trip home, through the day itself, to the quiet days after and the year-end check-in, and ends on the finale.
No sky dates on screen. Rows 15, 16, 18 and 19 were rewritten after b29's rows landed, so nothing repeats their
episodes 4 (coat pile, ten minutes outside), 5 (working at the holiday table), 7 (first-time hosting) and 12 (sweaters).

**Format (every film, 30 s = 25.6 s story + the 4.4 s default end card).** The shared look lives in
`promo/films/nhse13/scenes/survkit.js`; every other episode imports it from there.
- Bug top-left, matching b29's: a pixel thermos + "HOLIDAY SURVIVAL GUIDE" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, icy
  mint) + " · NO. n" (white 60%).
- 0–5.4 **SITUATION.** Frame 0: the hook headline is already up, a yellow "SITUATION" tape sticker under it, and the
  sign(s) mid-action in the episode's set.
- 5.4–17.4 **THE GUIDE.** A cream survival-manual page slides up (lower third) with three steps; each step gets 4 s, a
  vignette acting it out above the page, and a red tick when it lands. The top headline becomes "How to survive: …".
  Card cut candidates 9.4 / 13.4 / 17.4 (cardfx plays the middle one).
- 17.4–25.6 **THE STARS.** THE STARS pop in with one line of advice in the dialogue box; the set comes back resolved
  (the end rhymes with frame 0); the last line pops at ~21.4 and holds to the end card.
- Pisces cries in every film (somewhere in the set, and again at the end, happy tears).
- Tie-ins are light and only where noted (talk cards).
