star_advice trial reels, 15 s each (story 0–10.6, then the 4.4 s end card). Bug: "DEALBREAKER OR NOT?".
The shared format lives in `promo/films/ndbe01/scenes/dealkit.js`; every other episode imports it.

**Format (every episode):**
- **0.0–3.9 · the situation.** Frame 0: the bug, the situation headline already landed at the top, and the sign
  mid-action with its prop. Quirk episodes: the sign does the quirk and its date reacts. Dealbreaker episodes: the
  behaviour belongs to a faceless grey "date" (never a sign); the sign is the one deciding.
- **3.9 · card cut (flip). 3.9–6.1 · the vote.** "Dealbreaker… | or *not?*", two big buttons (red DEALBREAKER,
  green NOT), a needle swinging between them on a ticking clock, Pisces chewing a tissue.
- **6.1 · THE STARS' stamp** slams the verdict ("NOT A DEALBREAKER." green / "DEALBREAKER." red). Dealbreaker
  episodes add the chip "SAVED TO MY DEALBREAKERS" (a real app feature, light).
- **6.55 · THE STARS say the last line** while the finale plays. Pisces cries in every film (happy tears on a NOT,
  proud tears on a DEALBREAKER).

Verdict rule: quirks are NOT dealbreakers; values are (kindness, honesty, respect for a no, trust, curiosity,
showing up). The joke is on the situation, never on a sign.
