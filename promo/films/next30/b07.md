**The show.** THE STARS host (gold star, a pixel mic). Two signs stand at podiums with buzzers; Pisces sits in the front
row of the studio audience with a tissue box and cries every episode. Three rounds every time: **FINISH THE TEXT**,
**GREEN FLAG OR RED FLAG**, **WHAT WOULD YOU DO?** The running gag: someone always buzzes first, and the scoreboard
says **SPEED: 0 PTS**, because the point goes to the *kindest* answer, not the fastest. The prize is a talk card.
Bug: "STAR TALK GAME NIGHT" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, top-left). The shared set and timeline live in
`promo/films/ngne01/gamekit.js`; every episode imports it.

**The arc across the 24.** 12 episodes pair opposite signs (each of the 6 opposite pairs plays twice) and 12 pair signs
of the same element (each of the 12 same-element pairs plays once). **Batch b07 (eps 1–12)** plays every opposite pair
once (odd episodes) and all six fire and earth pairs (even episodes). **Batch b08 (eps 13–24)** has the six opposite
**rematches** and the six air and water pairs. Standings so far: ties in eps 1, 6, 11; wins for Sag (2, 12), Scorpio (3),
Virgo (4, 8), Gemini (5), Capricorn (7, 10), Aquarius (9).
