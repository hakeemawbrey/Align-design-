# b08: Star Talk Game Night, episodes 13–24 (`ngne13`–`ngne24`)

Series: **Star Talk Game Night** (star_talk · post · 36 s each, 31.6 s story + the 4.4 s end card). Episodes 13–24 are
the **same-element** half of the run: every one of the 12 same-element pairs plays once (fire, earth, air, water in
turn), so the opposite-sign half (eps 1–12) keeps its own pairings.

**The format (identical in all 12, shared look in `promo/films/ngne13/gamekit.js`):** THE STARS host from the centre of
the stage with a pixel mic; two contestants on podiums with buzzers and a three-star KINDNESS tally; a studio screen
shows the prompt; each answer arrives as a face-down card that flips (card first, face last). Bug top-left: "STAR TALK
GAME NIGHT · EP n". Round pips under the bug.
- 0.0–3.4 hook: the hook headline is up at frame 0, the contestants are mid-hop at their buzzers, the screen shows the
  matchup and element night; THE STARS' intro line.
- 3.4 / 10.5 / 17.6: ROUND 1 "Finish the text" · ROUND 2 "Green flag or red flag?" · ROUND 3 "What would you do?"
  (prompt 0.3 s in, a buzzer at 1.1, card A flips at 1.4, card B at 2.7, THE STARS' verdict at 3.5, "+1 ★ KIND" on the
  kinder card(s) at 5.4). Card cut at 17.6.
- 24.7 the winner: tallies count up, spotlight + confetti on the kindest (or a tie), THE STARS' last line, out by 31.5.
- Pisces cries in the front row of the audience every episode (tissue box, more tears every round, fountains at the
  winner); in the two Pisces episodes Pisces cries on the podium instead.
- The winner is whoever answers most kindly, never whoever buzzes first.
