## The First Three Dates (30 s, 3 dates × 4 elements) · bug "THE FIRST THREE DATES"
The format: three heart pips (DATE 1 · 2 · 3) under the bug, one lit per episode. Hook at t = 0: "Date *N* with a
*<element>* sign." with the date star and "you" mid-action at the spot. SKIP beat (the move that doesn't land, a red
✕ stamp), card cut (11.0), TRY beat (the move that does, THE STARS explain in 2 lines), then a pinned TIP note and the
last line from THE STARS. Pisces cries somewhere in each one. Date 1 = what to ask, date 2 = how to read the second date,
date 3 = when to say what you want. Light tie-ins: talk cards (date 1), a Pull reading (date 2), dealbreakers (date 3).
