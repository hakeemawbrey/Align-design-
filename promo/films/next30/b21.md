Format (shared kit `promo/films/nf16e01/scenes/familykit.js`, copied into every episode's folder): each episode opens
on a "DEAR STARS" question sticker from one sign (frame 0: the question headline + the asking star mid-hop), plays the

deck), and THE STARS say the last line. Pisces cries in every episode. One sign asks per episode, so eps 1–12 cover all
twelve signs once.

Arc for eps 1–12: the countdown from the invite to the first night at the table (deciding to go, getting ready, the
door, the dinner). Eps 13–24 (batch b22) can pick up from the table: the questions, the walk after, the drive home.
