# b47 — Wait for It, Volume 2 (1–12) and Silent Review, Volume 2 (1–12) (star_advice trial reels)

Both are trial reels for people who don't follow yet: the hook headline, the bug and the cast are on screen at t = 0
(no fade from black), and the first second reads on its own. Winter / holiday-season setting, no dates on screen.
Every film: card first, face last; Pisces cries; no "Align" in the last 2 s; the series name is the bug, top-left
("WAIT FOR IT…" / "SILENT REVIEW"); 18 s including the 2.4 s short end card; no Align card transition (owner change, 4 Nov).

Shared look lives in a kit inside each series' first film: `nwfe01/scenes/wait.js` (adapted from the Volume 1 kit in
trwaittaurus) and `nsre01/scenes/silentkit.js` (adapted from trsilentletter). The other eleven films of each series
import that file.
