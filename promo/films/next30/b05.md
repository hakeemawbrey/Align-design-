sign needs advice, and the chat (usually Virgo) has it. Account: star_talk · post · 20–30 s · bug "THE THREAD".
The shared look lives in `promo/films/nthe01/chatkit.js` (the chat window, message kinds, the stage strip, the bug);
episodes 2–16 import it from there.

message (the pinned banner carries over) · Capricorn posts an AGENDA · Gemini replies to three messages at once.
