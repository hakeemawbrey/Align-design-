**Follow promo/films/STARTALK4.md exactly** for everything except the 25 films below. **This batch goes bigger:** genre parodies and wild premises. Each film commits to its genre's look and sound (heist, horror, nature documentary, musical, superhero…), then lands one true, warm relationship lesson. The rules for the genre setting:
- Keep it pixel art, in the night sky.
- Recurring cast as before: Pisces cries in the background of every film.

**Usage cap:** the account has a 5-hour usage cap. Do at most two full sheets (one at rate 2, one final at rate 4) and use stills for spot checks.

## The 25
Each entry gives the folder, the title, the length, the music style and key, the premise and beats, the lesson, the tie-in and the last line. Keep the lesson's meaning, the tie-in, the length and the last line. Improve everything else.

1. **`stpirates`: "Pirates of the Heart." · 60 s · cinematic Gm**
   - **Premise:** Captain Scorpio's pirate crew sails the nebula with a treasure map to "THE PERFECT PARTNER". They pass islands of fake treasure: a six-pack island, a "rich" chest that's fool's gold, a fame lighthouse.
   - **Twist:** X marks a little island where a star is quietly making you tea on a rainy day.
   - **Lesson:** "The treasure is how they | treat you on an *ordinary* day." Chip: +1 PRIORITIES.
   - **Tie-in:** tonight's deck.
   - **Last line:** **"Drop *anchor*."**
2. **`stwestern`: "High Noon." · 45 s · lofi Am (a spaghetti-western whistle)**
