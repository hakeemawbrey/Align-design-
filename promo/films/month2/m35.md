# Month 2 boost · batch m35 · Zodiac Hot Seat, episodes 1–24 (star_advice trial reel, page 34)

Prefix `khs`. e01–e22 short (9–12 s), e23–e24 long (60 s). No end card, no card transition, no logo, and "Align" never
on screen. Shared look: `promo/films/khse01/hotkit.js` (imported read-only by khse02–e24), on top of
`promo/films/hookkit.js`.

**The show:** bug "ZODIAC HOT SEAT" top-left. One sign in a swivel chair under a hard white spotlight on a dark stage.
THE STARS (the off-screen host) fire rapid questions: each one slams in as a card at the top with a shrinking timer
ring, and the sign blurts the answer in a bubble. Three or four quick, funny ones, then the **last question** drops the
music out (sudden silence), the light softens, and the sign answers honestly: that is the relatable feeling. THE STARS
close with one warm line. ♓ Pisces runs the buzzer from a little booth bottom-right and cries in every film (it is the
show's whole thing: it cries at every honest answer). In e12 Pisces is in the chair and ♌ Leo runs the booth.
Trial reels: the hook line says the premise, so it works for someone who has never seen the account. Questions tease,
never shame; the joke is on the situation. e01–e12 one per sign; e13–e22 a themed round each (situationship, left on
read, 2 a.m. scroll…) for ten signs; e23 a full 60 s Aquarius interview; e24 all 12 signs in one chair (60 s).
