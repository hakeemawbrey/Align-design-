# m32 · Spot the Green Flag (star_advice trial reels, prefix ksg, page 33)

The format: **one sign, two takes of the same moment, side by side.** Take A (left) and Take B (right) play the same
relatable situation two ways, a 3-2-1 timer sits on the centre divider ("SPOT THE GREEN FLAG"), then the reveal: a
pixel green flag slams into the winning side with a ding, the other side drains to grey. Because it's the **same sign
in both takes**, the joke is always on the behaviour, never on a sign. Pisces is the judge in a little facecam at the
bottom of the divider and cries in every film (nervous sniffles during the timer, happy-tear fountains at the reveal).
THE STARS say the last line. The series bug "SPOT THE GREEN FLAG" top-left (Inter 800 small caps, green). No end card,
no card cut, no logo. The winning side alternates so nobody can guess (L/R noted per row). Trial reels must make sense
to a first-time viewer: the premise caption names the moment by second 2.

Short timeline (≈10 s): frame 0 is both takes mid-action + the hook line; 0–0.4 whip zoom (pattern break); 0.4–3.6
the two takes play (A then B); 3.6–6.3 the 3-2-1 timer (freeze, Pisces sweating); 6.3 reveal; 6.9 THE STARS' line;
hard cut (holds 0.6–1.0 s) or loop (eases back into the frame-0 composition).
Long (60 s): five rounds of ~10 s with ROUND chapter stickers, a running score, a promise up front, a final round that
pays it off.
