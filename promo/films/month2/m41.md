# m41 · Trends: Extended (star_talk posts, prefix kxtr, page 36)

Part 2 series `xtrends`: 12 long new episodes (all ~2 min) of the trend formats from Trends 1 (`tr*`) and Trends 2
(`nt2e*`). Each episode takes one trend format and tells the whole story instead of just the gag: same pixel-star cast,
night sky, bone caption stickers, Inter 800 + EB Garamond italic accents, Pisces crying somewhere in every film.
The series bug is the format name (top-left, Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced), e.g. "STORYTIME", as in the old films.
No end card, no card transition, no logo or CTA. `DURATION` is the story. Shared episode engine:
`promo/films/kxtre01/scenes/xkit.js` (chapters, captions, stickers, cast, sets), on top of the hook kit
(`promo/films/hookkit.js`) for the frame-0 hook effects, chapter stickers and the loop-back.

Every long film: frame 0 mid-chaos with the hook line on screen; a pattern break in the first second; the premise by
second 3; a payoff promised up front; chapter stickers; a new beat every 8–10 s; nothing static for more than 3 s; a
real ending that pays off the promise. Hard cuts hold the punchline 0.6–1.0 s; loops ease back into frame 0.
