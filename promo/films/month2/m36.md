# Month 2 boost · batch m36 · The Unsent Text, episodes 1–24 (star_advice trial, page 34)

Format: one sign, one phone, one feeling. The text is typed… deleted… typed… deleted (letters fly off the screen on
every backspace burst), and the last beat is ONE line from THE STARS on what to do with the feeling. Advice is warm
and healthy: sometimes the honest version gets sent, sometimes the feeling goes in a notebook, never a game.
Every film is a stranger-proof trial reel: the hook line names the situation at frame 0.

Series look (shared in `promo/films/kute01/unsentkit.js`, imported read-only by kute02–24): a big phone in the
centre with a chat header (the recipient's name, fictional, no app chrome), a draft field with a blinking cursor, a
keyboard whose keys light up as letters land, letters that fly off the screen on deletes, a DRAFT counter, the sign
star behind/next to the phone. Recurring bit: **Pisces "reads over your shoulder"** from a corner and sobs at every
deleted draft (when Pisces is the hero, the tears hit the screen). THE STARS' line types in the RPG dialogue box.
Bug top-left: THE UNSENT TEXT. No end card, no card transition, no logo, no season number, the joke is on the
situation, never on a sign. Each sign is the hero twice. Episodes 1–22 short (7–12 s), 23–24 long (60 s, chapter
stickers, a new draft every 8–10 s, payoff promised up front).

Frame-0 rule for all: the phone is already mid-delete (a backspace burst, letters mid-flight), the hero mid-reaction,
the hook line on screen. Pattern break inside the first second (record-scratch freeze or whip zoom onto the draft).
