# Month 2 boost · batch m09 · Zodiac Confessional, episodes 1–20 (star_talk post, page 25)

Format: a reality-show confessional booth. One sign at a time sits in the chair under a ring light, a velvet curtain
behind, a lower third with their name and a quote. Off-camera producer questions appear as white-on-black captions
("PRODUCER: do you like them?"). Cutaways are stamped EARLIER / B-ROLL with a REC frame. Bug "ZODIAC CONFESSIONAL"
(top-left, Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced) with a red REC dot. **Running gag: Pisces is the boom-mic operator,
dangling into the top of frame and crying at every confession** (happy or sad tears), so Pisces cries in every film.
Shared look: `promo/films/kcfe01/scenes/cfkit.js` (imported by kcfe02–kcfe20). Hook kit: `promo/films/hookkit.js`.
No end card, no card transition, no logo, no "Align" on screen. App features appear only as props (a talk card,
tonight's deck), never as an ad.
Episodes 1–15 are short (10–25 s); 16–20 are long (60–90 s): one reality-show "episode" with three signs, three
confessionals and one dramatic dinner, chapter stickers, a payoff promised up front.
