# m26 — Wait, That's Me (star_advice · trial reels) · kwme01–kwme24

Format: one hyper-relatable line on screen; the sign is already mid-thing at frame 0, a pattern break in the first
second (record scratch + freeze, colour flash, whip zoom), then the sign **slowly turns to the camera** (deadpan push-in),
a tiny last line, and the film **loops** back into frame 0 with the kit's loop-back helper. Pisces cries in every film
(a corner cameo when Pisces isn't the hero). No end card, no card cut, nothing after `DURATION`. Shorts 6–12 s,
eps 23–24 are the two 60 s tests. The joke is on the situation, never the sign. Shared format look lives in
`kwme01/wmkit.js` (read-only for the rest of the batch).
