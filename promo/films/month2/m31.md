# Month 2 boost · batch m31 · Fake Ad, episodes 1–24 (star_advice trial reel, page 33)

Prefix `kfa`. e01–e22 short (6–12 s), e23–e24 long (60 s). No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word
"Align" never on screen. Shared look: `promo/films/kfae01/adkit.js` (imported read-only by kfae02–e24), on top of
`promo/films/hookkit.js`.

**The show:** bug "FAKE AD" top-left. A loud late-night infomercial parody. Every ad opens on grainy black-and-white
**BEFORE** footage of one sign mid-disaster with a relatable dating feeling (frame 0, already mid-chaos, the hook line
slammed on screen: "Do YOU…?"). A record scratch / whip zoom flips the world into saturated infomercial colour:
"INTRODUCING…" and a fictional product box slams in (a silly invented name, starburst "NEW!"). The box opens and the
product is **the real, healthy tip** (an honest question, a pause, a kind sentence). The sign tries it, beams. ♓
**Pisces is the studio audience / testimonial**, sobbing "it changed my life" in every film (a lower third: "PISCES,
VERY MOVED"). The fine-print gag lands the last line ("PRICE: free. SIDE EFFECTS: peace."). The joke is on the
feeling, never on a sign; no fake stats (no "9 out of 10"), no real brands; product names are invented and neutral.
Every sign leads at least one short; Aries and Pisces lead once, the rest twice.
