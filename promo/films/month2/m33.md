# Month 2 boost · batch m33 · 5 Things Every ___ Needs to Hear (`fivethings`, star_advice trial reels)

24 films, prefix `k5t`, library page 33. Episodes 1–22 short (9.5–12 s), 23–24 long (60 s). Two films per sign, so
every sign is covered twice and fairly; the joke is on the situation, never the sign.

The format: frame 0 is the sign star mid-chaos with the hook line already on screen. In the first second a pattern
break (whip zoom, record scratch + freeze, colour flash or sudden silence) slams in the counter "5 THINGS". Then a
fast list: a giant number slams in with one line (6 words max) per beat, and the sign reacts with a tiny prop. Pisces
sits in a corner with a tissue box, sniffling at 1 and fountaining happy tears by 5. The bug is
"5 THINGS EVERY ___ NEEDS TO HEAR" with the sign's name in the blank. No end card, no card cut, no logo; the film ends
on a hard cut (last line holds ~0.8 s) or loops back into the frame-0 composition.

Long versions (60 s): the same crazy first second and a promise up front ("number 5 is the one"), then each thing is
a ~10 s chapter (chapter sticker, the line, a mini-scene with THE STARS and a gag every 8–10 s), and number 5 pays off
the promise.
