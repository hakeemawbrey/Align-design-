# m42 · Silent Review: Extended (kxsr, 6 × 60 s) + Rate My Date Idea: Extended (kxrd, 6 × 75 s) · star_talk posts · page 36

Both series continue the old ones (Silent Review: trsilent*, nsre*; Rate My Date Idea: nrde*): the same desk, fairy
lights, closed captions, verdict card and stamp, the same sign stars, the same pink bug. New for Month 2: no end card,
no card transition, no logo; frame 0 is mid-chaos with the hook line on screen; chapter stickers from hookkit.js; a new
beat every 8–10 s; the payoff is promised in the first 3 s. Kits: `kxsre01/srkit.js` and `kxrde01/rdkit.js` (the old
kits import endcard.js / cardfx.js, so the format pieces are copied into the new kits instead of imported; the shared
modules startalk.js, lesson.js, sky.js and hookkit.js are imported read-only).

**Silent Review: Extended (60 s):** a sign silently reviews a whole date, course by course. Faces only, closed
captions narrate the face ("[ one eyebrow ]"). Chapters: COURSE 1–4, a mini score card slams between courses, then
THE VERDICT card (four rows + a stamped total) and the last line. Pisces is the server at every venue and cries at
every course (in kxsre05 Pisces is the reviewer and cries alone).

**Rate My Date Idea: Extended (75 s):** the pitch sticker ("Rate my *date idea:*" + the idea in quotes), then the date
is actually played out in chapters (THE PLAN, three stops, BUT…, THE SAVE), then THE STARS' rubber stamp, the italic
word and the last line. Pisces gives every idea 10/10 and cries throughout (in kxrde06 Pisces is the pitcher).
