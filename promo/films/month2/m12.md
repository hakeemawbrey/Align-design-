# Month 2 boost · batch m12 · Text Rescue, episodes 1–20 (star_advice post, page 26)

Shared look: `promo/films/ktre01/rescuekit.js` (imported read-only by ktre02–ktre20), built on `hookkit.js`.
Bug "TEXT RESCUE" top-left (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, rescue-red) with a pixel life ring. The set is one
big pixel phone in the middle of the night sky: the thread at the top, the **draft** in the compose bar. Every rescue
goes the same way: the cringe draft is already typed at frame 0 with the thumb over SEND, a **red-pen slash** (record
scratch + freeze) kills it, THE STARS (gold star with a red pen) say why in one line, the honest, kind rewrite types
in gold and sends, and the reply lands. The sender is a sign character below the phone; Pisces reads over the
phone's shoulder and cries in every film. The rewrites are honest and kind: never games, tests or tracking.
No end card, no card transition, no logo, CTA or the brand name. Lengths are story lengths. Shorts 15–30 s, longs 60–90 s
with chapter stickers ("RESCUE 1/5" …) and a new draft, slash or reply every 8–10 s.
