# Month 2 boost · batch m27 · Which One Are You? (star_advice trial reels, kwo, page 31)

24 films: kwoe01–kwoe22 short (8–11 s), kwoe23–kwoe24 long (60 s). No end card, no card transition, no logo.

**The format.** Four signs in a 2 × 2 grid, numbered 1–4, all doing the same relatable thing four different ways.
Frame 0: the grid is already mid-action and mid-zoom (whip zoom out of one panel), the hook line (the situation,
7 words max) is slammed at the top, and the bug "WHICH ONE ARE YOU?" sits top-left. Each panel gets its spotlight
beat with a one-line caption; then "COMMENT YOUR NUMBER" slams in, and Pisces always wedges into the middle of the grid
as a sobbing number 5, with a different line every film (that's the last line). Pisces is never one of the four, so
the other eleven signs each appear 8 times across the 22 shorts, and every option is played sympathetically: the joke
is on the situation, never on a sign.
Format kit: `promo/films/kwoe01/scenes/wokit.js` (imported read-only by every kwo film), on top of hookkit.js.

**Long versions (60 s).** "Keep count. Your verdict's at the end." Six rounds (chapter stickers), each a 2 × 2 in which
number N is always the same type (a different sign each round), a tally HUD, and a verdict at the end: "Mostly 1s…",
with one warm, healthy line of advice for each type. Pisces is number 5 in every round, crying harder each time.
