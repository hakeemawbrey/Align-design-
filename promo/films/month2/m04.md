# m04: Brain vs Heart, episodes 1–20 (kbhe01–kbhe20)

Series **Brain vs Heart** · account star_talk · kind post · library page 24 · prefix `kbh`.
Shorts e01–e15 (12–16 s), longs e16–e20 (76–86 s). (Len column updated to the built lengths.) No end card, no card transition, no logo, "Align" never on screen.

**The format.** We are inside one sign's head: a little purple control room. The big curved window at the top is the
sign's eyes (what they see right now: usually their phone). Below it, the console with one giant button for the
decision. Two tiny pixel characters fight over it: **BRAIN** (pink, round glasses, clipboard, wants to be careful) and
**HEART** (red, tiny arms, zero chill, wants to do it NOW). A scoreboard in the room keeps count (BRAIN 0 · 0 HEART).
The joke is always on the situation; both of them are right about something, and the ending is the healthy middle.
Bug top-left: "BRAIN VS HEART" + "INSIDE: ♈ ARIES" (the head we're in).

**Pisces cries in every film:** the **Pisces reaction cam**, a little rounded corner window (bottom-right) where Pisces
watches the whole thing and cries (worried tears, then happy tears). In e12 we are inside Pisces' own head, and the
cam shows THE STARS instead while Pisces cries on the window.

**The kit:** `promo/films/kbhe01/bhkit.js` (the room, Brain, Heart, the window, the phone, bubbles, scoreboard, the
Pisces cam, the bug, referee for the longs); e02–e20 import it from there. Hook effects come from
`promo/films/hookkit.js` (whip zoom, record scratch/freeze, shake, slam title, notification drop, loading bar,
countdown, chapter sticker, achievement pop, loop-back).

Longs: THE STARS is the referee (striped shirt, whistle), each round is a chapter sticker, the payoff is promised up
front ("wait for the final round"), a new beat every 8–10 s, and the last round pays it off.
