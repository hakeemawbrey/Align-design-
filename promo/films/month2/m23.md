## Zodiac Lie Detector (kld) — kit in `promo/films/klde01/ldkit.js`
- **Set:** a dark interrogation room, one hanging lamp, a sign strapped into a chair with two pixel electrodes (wires
  to the machine), the polygraph on a desk with a paper roll and a needle tracing live (the trace gets taller with
  every lie), a red alarm light, a LIE / TRUTH stamp. THE STARS ask the questions (dialogue box). ♓ Pisces is the
  operator at the machine with a tissue box, already crying at frame 0 (in e12 Pisces is in the chair and ♍ Virgo
  operates, while Pisces cries through every answer).
- **Frame 0 = thumbnail:** the needle mid-scribble off the chart, paper flying, the alarm red, the subject mid-scream,
  the quoted lie on screen (≤ 7 words). In the first second: a record scratch + freeze ("let's run that back"), then
  the question, the answer, the needle. Premise lands by 3 s.
- **Shorts (e01–e09):** question → the lie → needle off the chart + LIE stamp → the truth (a flash cutaway) → one
  follow-up that comes back TRUTH (the funny honest answer) → THE STARS' last line. Joke on the situation, never the sign.
- **Longs (e10–e12, 60 s):** "10 questions. Wait for the verdict." Chapter stickers (WARM-UP / THE CRUSH / THE BIG
  ONE / VERDICT), a question every ~4.5 s, the needle and the alarm louder each lie, a running LIES/TRUTHS counter,
  and a verdict that pays it off.

## Long Distance Notes (kln) — kit in `promo/films/klne01/ldnkit.js`
- **Set:** a vertical split screen, two halves with their own sky (sun or night for their real local time), a city
  name + a live clock badge on each half, a pixel skyline, one star in each half; phones, notes and props cross the
  split line. THE STARS give the one small habit (dialogue box). ♓ Pisces cries in every film: as a partner (e04, e10)
  or as the roommate on one side, crying happy tears at the habit.
- **Time zones are real for Nov–Dec** (winter time in the north, summer time in Sydney and Auckland).
- **Frame 0:** both halves mid-action, a notification or call mid-drop, the hook line on screen. A whip/flash pattern
  break in the first second, both clocks slam in, premise by 3 s.
- **Shorts (e01–e09, 20–24 s):** the feeling → THE STARS' one small habit → the habit played out on both halves →
  the payoff and the last line.
- **Longs (e10–e12, 60 s):** "a day apart in 60 seconds", chapter stickers (MORNING / MIDDAY / EVENING / NIGHT /
  ARRIVALS), a new habit or beat every ~8 s, and the two halves meet at the end (the split line slides away).
