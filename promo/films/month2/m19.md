# Month 2 boost · batch m19 · The Almost Relationship, episodes 1–20 (kare01–kare20)

star_advice posts. Library page 29. 15 short (15–30 s, e01–e15) + 5 long (60–90 s, e16–e20).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, the brand name never on screen. `DURATION` is the story length.
The bug (top-left, Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced): THE ALMOST RELATIONSHIP.
Pisces cries in every film (as the friend on the couch, or as a lead). THE STARS (gold narrator star) give the advice
in the RPG dialogue box. The joke is on the situation, never on a sign. Advice: honest, kind, never a test or a game.

**Format (short):** frame 0 is mid-chaos with the hook line (≤ 7 words) already on screen; a hook-kit pattern break in
the first second; by 3 s the situationship is named. Two or three quick "almost" beats (the evidence), then the turn:
THE STARS say how to ask (or how to hear the answer). Last line in the box. Hard cut (punchline holds 0.6–1.0 s) or a
loop (the last 0.3–0.5 s eases back into the frame-0 composition with the kit's loop helper).
**Format (long):** a mini-drama of one situationship in chapter stickers (the vibe → the evidence → the avoiding →
the ask → the answer), a payoff promised up front, a new beat every 8–10 s, then how to ask and how to hear it.
Shared look: `kare01/scenes/almostkit.js` (rooms, phone, couples, Pisces + tissues, sticker, bug, advice box);
the hook effects come from `promo/films/hookkit.js` (read-only).
