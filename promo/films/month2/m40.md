# m40 · Remix Cities: Extended (kxrce01–08) + Then vs Now: Extended (kxtve01–08) · star_talk posts · page 36

All 16 are long (60 s), Part 2 episodes of old series: new stories, not re-edits. No end card, no card cut, no logo,
no CTA, the word "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` is the story. Pisces cries in every film. Chapter stickers, the
promise and the frame-0 effects come from `promo/films/hookkit.js` (read-only).

**Remix Cities: Extended** continues *Remix* (srcity*) and *Snow Globe Cities* (nsge*): THE STARS (the gold narrator
star) are your tour guide through one city's whole night out, sign by sign, with the dialogue box, real local touches
(neighbourhoods, landmarks, food, weather; no businesses or brands) and a light winter feel. Shared look in
`promo/films/kxrce01/rckit.js`: bug "REMIX CITIES" (top-left, small caps), a ROUTE strip under it (the night's stops as
pins, a dot that travels, the clock), one full-frame street/interior per stop, the dialogue box at the bottom.
New cities only (none from the first Remix set or the Snow Globe sets).

**Then vs Now: Extended** continues *Then vs Now* (yr*) and *Then vs Now: Holidays* (nhve*): the same evening, 2016 on
top (the old-phone grade from `nhve01/scenes/thennow.js`: soft, warm, grainy, jittery, the pixel date stamp) and 2026
underneath (clean, glowing), played side by side, hour by hour. Shared look in `promo/films/kxtve01/tvkit.js`: bug
"THEN vs NOW", the caption "[thing] · *2016* vs *2026*", a clock chip on the seam per chapter, the active half pops while
the other dims a little; the 2016 star is cringe-cute, never mocked, and the 2026 star isn't perfect either.

Endings: hard cut = the punchline holds 0.6–1.0 s and the last frame is a story frame; loop = the last 0.4 s eases back
into the exact frame-0 composition (hookkit loop helper).
