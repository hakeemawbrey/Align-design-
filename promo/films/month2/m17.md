# Month 2 boost · batch m17 · Say It Better, episodes 1–20 (star_advice post, library page 28)

The format: a fight is already mid-scream at frame 0. The bad line ("You NEVER…", "Calm down", "I'm not mad") sits in a
huge comic bubble, a record scratch freezes it, a red pen crosses it out, THE STARS say why it backfires, and the
better line writes itself in. The other sign actually hears it and the fight turns. Warm, never preachy: the joke is on
the line, never on a sign. Eps 1–12 give every sign one turn as the one who learns; 13–15 are bonus pairs.
Eps 16–20 are long (75–90 s): **the same argument played twice**, ROUND 1 the bad way, a VHS REWIND, ROUND 2 the better
way, then THE VERDICT with score cards (chapter stickers from the hook kit, a new beat every 8–10 s).

Shared look: `promo/films/ksbe01/scenes/sbkit.js` (imported read-only by ksbe02–ksbe20): a warm pixel apartment set
(living room, kitchen, bedroom, party), the comic bubble, the red-pen strike, the better-line write-on, a small
"SAY IT BETTER" bug (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, top-left), THE STARS narrate in the dialogue box.
Pisces cries in every film (a lead in 6 and 12, a crying neighbour/friend in the others). No end card, no card cut,
no logo. Props only from real features (talk cards, tonight's deck) and only as objects.
