# m37: Rate the Opener (star_advice · trial) · kroe01–kroe24 · library page 35

Series key `opener`, prefix `kro`. Episodes 1–22 short (9–11 s), 23–24 long (60 s).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word never appears on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

Shared format look: `promo/films/kroe01/openerkit.js` (imported read-only by kroe02–kroe24), built on
promo/films/hookkit.js.
- Bug top-left "RATE THE OPENER" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** a giant chat bubble with the first message is mid-flight out of a phone toward the lens
  (motion streaks, tilted), the sender sign is mid-reaction beside it (mid-scream / mid-cringe / mid-swoon), a row of
  judges' score paddles is mid-flip at the bottom, and the hook line (≤7 words) is slammed on screen.
- **Pattern break at ~0.4 s:** record scratch + freeze (with the red "caught" circle on the opener), or a whip zoom into
  the bubble.
- **By 3 s:** THE STARS' paddle lands the first score with a one-word reason ("vague", "too soon", "a lot").
- **Then:** a red pen slashes the opener and THE STARS write the better one (honest, warm, curious — never a tactic);
  the paddles flip to the new score with a ding, the reply arrives, and THE STARS' last line lands big.
- **Pisces cries in every film:** ♓ Pisces is the judge on the end of the panel, sobbing into a tissue (tears level by
  film), or the sender itself.
- **Endings:** *loop* = the last 0.4 s eases back into the exact frame-0 composition (kit loop helper); *hard cut* = the
  punchline holds 0.6–1.0 s, last frame is a story frame.
- Scores are joke scores (x/10). The joke is on the opener (the situation), never on the sign. Every opener rewrite
  is something a kind person could actually send.
