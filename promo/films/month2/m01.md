# Month 2 boost · batch m01 · Don't Watch This If You're a ___ (star_talk post, page 23)

Series key `dontwatch`, prefix `kdw`. Episodes 1–15 short (10–25 s), 16–20 long (60–90 s).
No end card, no card transition, no logo. `DURATION` = story length.

Shared look: `promo/films/kdwe01/dwkit.js` (imported read-only by kdwe02–e20, built on promo/films/hookkit.js).
- Bug top-left "DON'T WATCH THIS" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, red).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** the sign's star is lunging at the lens, mid-slap, one point pressed flat against the glass
  (smudged, shaking), a red REC frame, and the hook line slammed on screen: "<SIGN>. Scroll. *Now.*"-style.
- **Pattern break at ~0.5 s:** record scratch + freeze, then a whip zoom out: the star "gets pulled off" the lens and
  we see the secret it didn't want us to see.
- **Pisces is the one filming** (a "📼 PISCES CAM" chip, its fins wobble on the frame edge). Every film Pisces ends up
  sobbing on screen (tears level 2–3), happy or sad tears.
- **Endings:** *loop* = the sign notices the camera again and lunges back into the frame-0 slap; *hard cut* = the
  punchline holds 0.6–1.0 s, last frame is a story frame.
- 12 signs covered; the joke is always on the situation (the secret is the relatable feeling), never the sign.
