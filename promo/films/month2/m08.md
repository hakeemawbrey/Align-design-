# m08 — Overthinking Simulator, episodes 1–20 (star_talk posts)

Format: a sign's brain runs OVERTHINK.SIM, a fictional retro computer program, on one tiny dating moment. A loading bar
crawls, every possible meaning gets rendered as a little "simulation" panel and then DELETED, the CPU overheats, then
reality answers with something tiny and normal. Shared look in `kose01/scenes/simkit.js` (the other 19 import it), on
top of the shared `hookkit.js`. Episodes 1–15 short, 16–20 long with chapter stickers.

- **t = 0 (thumbnail):** mid-chaos. The sim window is already open with the loading bar mid-glitch, the sign mid-scream
  or mid-freak-out, and the hook line (≤ 7 words) slammed on screen. Bug: OVERTHINKING SIMULATOR, top-left.
- **First second:** a pattern break (whip zoom, record scratch + freeze, colour flash or sudden silence).
- **By second 3:** the premise (the tiny thing they're overthinking) has landed.
- No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. Endings are a hard cut (punchline held
  ~0.8 s) or a seamless loop back to frame 0.
- Pisces cries in every film (as the sign, or as the sobbing "tech support" in the corner).
- The joke is on the situation, never on a sign. App features only as props (a talk card, tonight's deck).
