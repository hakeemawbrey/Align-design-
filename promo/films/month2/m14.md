# Month 2 boost · batch m14 · The 10-Second Pep Talk, episodes 1–20 (star_advice post, page 27)

Format: a best-friend sign grabs the camera and gives the viewer's sign the pep talk they need right now. Frame 0 is
the friend mid-lunge into the lens (whip zoom, hook line already up). By second 3 the feeling has landed (a caption
naming it plus a quick flash of the spiral: the phone, the bed, the mirror). Then three short, warm lines, each one
stamped on screen while the listener perks up, and a last line that lands big. Pisces cries in every film (in the
corner holding tissues, or as a coach/listener; happy tears count).
Shared series look: `promo/films/kpte01/pepkit.js` (the lunge, the spiral scenes, the pep lines, the stamp, Pisces),
imported read-only by kpte02–20. Hook effects from `promo/films/hookkit.js` (read-only).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, no season number. The joke is on the situation, never on a sign; the advice
is warm (honesty, boundaries, self-kindness), never controlling.
Episodes 1–15 short (17–18 s: the grab, the spiral, a real 10-second stopwatch pep talk, the last line), 16–20 long (69–73 s, "the full pep talk" at 2 a.m., chapter stickers, a new beat every
8–10 s, the payoff promised up front: "stay for the last line").
