confetti for #1). Shared look for the long version: `promo/films/kxrke01/scenes/rankfull.js` (imported read-only by

- 0–4 s hook: frame 0 is mid-crash (the twelve "? ? ?" plates mid-flight off the board after a record scratch), the
  hook line big, a promise sticker "WAIT FOR #1". By 3 s the premise has landed.
- Chapter stickers: THE BOTTOM FOUR (#12–#9) · THE MIDDLE (#8–#5) · THE PODIUM (#4–#2) · NUMBER ONE.
- Every rank is a 6 s beat: the row slams in, the sign hops centre stage, says its reason in a bubble, then a prop gag
  pops at ~3 s (phone, clock, stamp, meter, tissues…). A new beat every 6 s, nothing static over 3 s.
- Drumroll, #1 with crown and confetti, a twist that pays off the promise, THE STARS' last line, hard cut.

**Your Sign at the Party: Extended** continues *Your Sign at the Holiday Party* (npye01–12: the cozy room, string
lights, snowy window, party hats, the pink bug, numbered caption stickers). It imports the npye01 kit's drawing helpers
(room, hats, tissues, cookies, clock, mic) read-only. Shared long-format look: `promo/films/kxpye01/scenes/partyfull.js`.
- One party from arrival to the last goodbye with all 12 signs: 0–4 s crazy hook + promise, then six chapters of two
  sign moments each (about 7 s per moment: a cut, the sign forward and big, its line, then a twist at ~3.5 s), the
  other signs milling in the background the whole time, then the goodbye and the last line.
