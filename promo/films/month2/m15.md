# m15 · Healing Era, episodes 1–20 (khee01–khee20) · star_advice post · library page 27

Series bug: "HEALING ERA" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, top-left). No season numbers, no end card, no card
transition, no logo, no CTA. `DURATION` is the story. Shorts are 20–26 s (eps 1–15), longs 60–90 s (eps 16–20).

**The format.** Every film opens on the series' signature hook: a sign on a floor after a breakup (the kitchen floor
first and most often), already mid-action at frame 0, a tear-off **DAY N** calendar pinned top-right and the hook line
(≤ 7 words) on screen. In the first second a pattern break from the shared hook kit (record scratch + freeze, whip
zoom, colour flash or sudden silence) lands the premise by 2–3 s. Then THE STARS (the warm narrator) or a friend gives
one honest, kind piece of advice, the sign tries one small healthy step, the calendar flips forward, and the film ends
on its last line, either a **hard cut** (the line lands and holds ~0.8 s) or a **loop** (the last ~0.4 s eases back into
the exact frame-0 composition). Pisces cries in every film (background sniffles with tissues, happy tears at the end, or
Pisces is the lead). The joke is on the situation, never on a sign; the advice is never controlling, never "win the
breakup", never jealousy games. App features appear only as props (a talk card on the fridge in a few films), never
named. Shared look: `promo/films/khee01/healkit.js` (kitchen set, floor, DAY calendar, HEALING… meter), importing the
shared hook kit read-only and the Single and Thriving prop helpers (`nste01/singlekit.js`) read-only.

Longs (16–20) are "30 days in 60–90 seconds": the same crazy first second, a payoff promised up front ("wait for day
30"), chapter stickers (WEEK 1 … DAY 30), a new beat every 8–10 s (a bad day, the friend who shows up, a setback, a
first laugh, the first good morning), and they end back on the day-1 kitchen floor, now smiling, looping into frame 0.
