# m30 — Guess the Sign, episodes 1–24 (star_advice trial reels)

Format: a game-show stage at night. A **black silhouette** of a sign star (no glyph, no colour, just a rim light) is
caught mid-action doing one hyper-relatable dating/texting/single-life thing. Clue chips drop in (CLUE 1, CLUE 2),
a buzzer countdown 3·2·1 runs, and the silhouette floods with its colour, glyph and name slam: the reveal comes at the
very end, so people watch again. One warm, honest last line, then the film **loops** back to the silhouette at frame 0
(or hard-cuts on the line). Shared look in `kgse01/scenes/guesskit.js` (the other 23 import it, read-only), on top of
the shared `hookkit.js`. Episodes 1–22 short (6–12 s); 23–24 long (60 s), six rounds each, with chapter stickers.

- **t = 0 (thumbnail):** mid-chaos. The silhouette is already mid-action (mid-scream, mid-fall, mid-spin), a big "?"
  on its chest, the stage lights mid-flash, the hook line (≤ 7 words) slammed on screen. Bug: GUESS THE SIGN, top-left.
- **First second:** a pattern break (whip zoom, record scratch + freeze, colour flash or sudden silence).
- **By second 3:** the relatable thing the silhouette is doing has landed.
- **Pisces cries in every film:** Pisces is the contestant at the buzzer in the corner, sobbing at every round (happy
  tears count). When the answer *is* Pisces, Pisces cries harder ("it's ME").
- **Fair to all 12:** every sign is the answer at least twice across the batch (shorts once each + ten seconds, the two
  longs cover six signs each). The joke is on the situation, never on a sign.
- No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. Endings: seamless loop (the last ~0.4 s
  eases back to the frame-0 silhouette) or hard cut (last line held ~0.8 s).
- App features only as props where they fit (a talk card, tonight's deck). No real brands, apps or platforms.
