## Deep Dive: ___ in Love (star_advice posts, prefix kdd, all long, about 2 min)

The format: one sign per film, a warm, honest "field guide" to loving them, in four chapters with chapter stickers:
**1 HOW THEY FLIRT · 2 WHAT THEY NEED · 3 HOW THEY FIGHT · 4 WHAT MAKES THEM STAY**. THE STARS narrate in a dialogue
box; the sign acts every beat out with a partner (a neutral sky-coloured star). Every chapter has 3 beats (a new gag
or reveal every 8–10 s), a "field note" card that slams in with the takeaway, and Pisces in the corner as the
"sobbing research assistant" with a tissue box. A small sticker in chapter 1 says "astrology = a lens, not a lab
result" (framed as a lens, never science). Payoff promised up front ("chapter four is the one"), paid off in
chapter 4. Shared look lives in `kdde07/scenes/ddkit.js` (kdde08–12 import it), on top of hookkit.js. Episodes
1–6 (Aries–Virgo) are batch m24; this batch takes 7–12 in zodiac order: Libra → Pisces.

- **t = 0 (thumbnail):** the sign is already mid-action, the hook line (≤ 7 words) slammed on screen, the bug
  DEEP DIVE: ___ IN LOVE top-left. **First second:** a whip zoom or record-scratch freeze + colour flash.
  **By second 3:** "everything nobody tells you about loving a ___" has landed.
- No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. Hard cut on the last line (held
  ~0.8 s) or a seamless loop into frame 0.
- Props only as objects: a talk card, tonight's deck, a dealbreaker card, a Push / Pull / Align reading.
