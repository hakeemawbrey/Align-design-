# Month 2 boost · batch m05 · Breaking News: The Stars (star_talk post, prefix kbn, episodes 01–20)

Series look: a fake late-night news show, built in `kbne01/newskit.js` and shared across all 20 films.
- THE STARS (gold star) anchors the desk.
- ♓ Pisces is the co-anchor and cries at every story (happy tears count).
- Other parts: a red BREAKING slam banner, a scrolling ticker, LIVE footage boxes, lower thirds and field reporters.
- Bug, top-left: BREAKING NEWS: THE STARS.
- No end card, no card cut and no logo. Every film ends on a hard cut or a loop back to frame 0.
- Episodes 01–15 are short (10–25 s); 16–20 are long (60–90 s) "full news hours" with chapter stickers.
- Relatable feelings: texting, left on read, exes, situationships, being single, crushes.
- 12-sign coverage: each sign leads one of the shorts 01–12, the multi-sign stories cover the rest, and the joke is
  always on the situation.
