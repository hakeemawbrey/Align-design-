# Month 2 boost, batch m06: Zodiac Courtroom (star_talk post), kzce01–kzce20

Series bug top-left: **ZODIAC COURTROOM**. The court is the same in every film: a wood-panel bench on the night sky,
THE STARS (the gold narrator star) as the judge in a tiny curled wig with a gavel, a jury box of small sign stars in
the gallery, and ♓ Pisces always in the jury, always crying (sad, shocked or happy tears). The charge sits on a court
ticker, evidence comes in a labelled evidence bag, and the verdict is a slammed stamp: GUILTY / NOT GUILTY / CASE
DISMISSED, then a kind, healthy **sentence** ("sentenced to: one honest text"). The joke is on the situation, never
on the sign; every verdict lands on behaviour.
No end card, no card cut, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. Frame 0: the gavel is mid-slam (or the jury is
mid-gasp) with the hook line already up. First second: a pattern break (whip zoom, record scratch / freeze, slam).
Long films (e16–e20): chapter stickers (OPENING STATEMENTS · EVIDENCE · SURPRISE WITNESS · CLOSING · THE VERDICT), the
payoff promised up front ("wait for the verdict"), a new beat every 8–10 s, and the ex as the surprise witness.
