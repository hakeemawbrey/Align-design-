# m29 · This or That: Love Edition (star_advice trial reels, prefix ktt, page 32)

The format: rapid A/B love questions. Two big tilted pixel cards fill the frame (THIS on the left in pink, THAT on the
right in blue) with a VS spark between them and a shrinking speed-timer bar on top. The sign stands at the bottom
centre and picks at speed: it slams into one card (gold check stamp), the other card goes flying off-screen, the next
pair whips in. The last pair is always the twist: a harder, truer question, answered with something warm and healthy
(advice account). The series bug top-left ("THIS OR THAT · LOVE EDITION", Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced).
No end card, no card cut, no logo. Pisces cries in every film (in a corner "tissue cam" when it isn't the hero).
Frame 0: the sign already mid-slam into the first card, the hook line on screen, the timer flashing.
e01–e12: one sign each (all 12). e13–e18: two-sign duels (split picks). e19–e22: all 12 signs vote (no numbers, just
characters running to a side). e23–e24: long 60-second versions with chapter stickers and a promised payoff.
Trial reels: every film explains itself with no prior context (the question is always on the cards).
