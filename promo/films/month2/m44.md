name tag "♈ YOUR STAR") speaks to YOU, warm, honest, a little cheeky. Each episode is the full guide for one sign,

pattern break inside the first second. Kit for the series: `promo/films/kxsge01/sgxkit.js` (built in e01, read-only
for the rest). Traits are "how astrology describes" the sign; no compatibility verdicts.
