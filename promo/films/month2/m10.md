# Month 2 boost · batch m10 · First Date Bingo, episodes 1–20 (star_talk post, page 26)

Prefix `kfb`. e01–e15 short (10–25 s), e16–e20 long (60–90 s). No end card, no card transition, no logo.
Shared look: `promo/films/kfbe01/bingokit.js` (imported read-only by kfbe02–e20), on top of `promo/films/hookkit.js`.

**The show:** bug "FIRST DATE BINGO" top-left. Two star characters on a first date (table, bench, lane…), and a big
pixel bingo card that fills live: each square gets slammed with a dauber stamp the moment it happens. A line of three
(shorts, 3×3 card with a free centre) or four (longs, 4×4) = BINGO slam. ♓ Pisces is the show's **bingo host** in a
little corner booth with a ball cage and a dauber, and cries in every film (it's the host's whole thing: it cries at
every BINGO, good or awkward). The joke is always on the first-date situation, never on a sign; every card ends warm.
12 signs each lead one short (e01–e12); e13–e15 and the longs are pairings.
