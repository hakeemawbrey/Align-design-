# Month 2 boost · batch m45 · Ask the Stars: Extended + Friends First: Extended (star_advice posts, page 37)

Series `xask` (prefix `kxas`, eps 1–8, all long, ~75 s) and `xfriends` (prefix `kxff`, eps 1–4, all long, ~60 s).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

**Shared look:** `promo/films/kxase01/xkit.js` (imported read-only by every film in this batch). It is built on
promo/films/hookkit.js and reuses the old kits read-only: the Ask the Stars Volume 2 look (askkit: snowy night sky,
"ASK THE STARS" bug, the anonymous DM question card, the answering sign's star, the astrology chip) and the Friends
First look (friendskit: sets, couch/café/kitchen, phones, the CHECK-IN card, the "FRIENDS FIRST" bug).
- **Ask the Stars: Extended (75 s):** frame 0 = the answering sign's star already mid-chaos with the hook line slammed
  on screen and the DM question buzzing in. Pattern break by 1 s (record scratch / freeze / whip zoom). Promise up
  front ("wait for the answer"). Five chapter stickers: 1 THE QUESTION · 2 WHY IT HAPPENS · 3 THE STORY ·
  4 WHAT HELPS · 5 THE ANSWER. A new beat every 8–10 s. THE STARS give the warm, honest answer; a short story
  (two stars) shows it working. Astrology is a lens (the chip), never science.
- **Friends First: Extended (60 s):** a full friendship check-in. Chapters: 1 THE SITUATION · 2 THE URGE ·
  3 THE CHECK-IN · 4 THE HARD PART · 5 AFTER. The CHECK-IN card carries the words to send.
- **Pisces cries in every film** (cameo with tears in the Ask films, a friend or the hero in Friends First).
- Heroes cover all 12 signs once across the batch; the joke is on the situation, never on a sign.
- App features only as props: a talk card (ep 5, ep 8, ff 3). Nothing else.
