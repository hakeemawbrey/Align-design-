# Month 2 boost · batch m21 · Mirror Talk, episodes 1–20 (star_advice post, page 29)

Format: a sign talks to its reflection, and the reflection talks back with the advice they'd give a friend. Set: a
pixel vanity mirror with bulb lights on a dark bedroom wall. The sign stands in front, three-quarter to us; the
reflection inside the glass is the same character, calmer, with a soft cyan rim (the "friend voice"). Dialogue box
name tags: the sign (e.g. ♈︎ ARIES) and THE REFLECTION. Recurring bit: Pisces sits on the vanity counter next to a
tissue box and cries at every heart-to-heart (in e12 Pisces is the lead). The "MIRROR TALK" bug top-left.
Shared series look: `promo/films/kmte01/mirrorkit.js` (mirror, room, reflection, Pisces counter cameo, props),
imported read-only by kmte02–20, plus the shared hook kit `promo/films/hookkit.js`.
No end card, no card transition, no logo, no season number. The joke is on the situation, never on a sign.
Episodes 1–15 short (15–30 s), 16–20 long (60–90 s: payoff promised up front, chapter stickers, a beat every 8–10 s).
