# m22 — One Card, Two People, episodes 1–20 (star_advice posts)

Format: two signs at a little café table, one **talk card** face down between them. Pisces is the card dealer at the
end of the table (in an apron, with a tissue box) and cries at every reveal; in the episodes where Pisces is one of the
two players, they cry as a player too. The card flips dramatically, the question slams up, both answer face down (the
real feature: neither answer shows until both have played), the answers flip together, a reaction gag, then THE STARS
give one warm line on **why that question brings people closer**, and the last line.

Every question is a real card from the app's talk-card deck (`app/src/data/talkCards.ts`), quoted as written. The card
is a story prop (an object on the table), never an ad; the brand name is never printed. The answers are the
characters' own, not the deck's sample answers.

Shared look: `koce01/scenes/cardkit.js` (table, chairs, dealer, the card flip, answer slots, the reveal, THE STARS
chip), on top of the shared `hookkit.js`. Episodes 1–15 short (15–30 s), 16–20 long (60–90 s: three cards in one
night, the third changes things; chapter stickers CARD 1 / CARD 2 / CARD 3; payoff promised up front).

- **t = 0 (thumbnail):** mid-chaos. The card is already mid-flip in the air over the table, both players mid-reaction
  (gasp, lunge, scream), Pisces mid-sob, and the hook line (≤ 7 words) slammed on screen. Bug top-left: ONE CARD, TWO
  PEOPLE.
- **First second:** a pattern break (whip zoom, record scratch + freeze, colour flash or sudden silence).
- **By second 3:** the card's question is on screen.
- No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. Endings are a hard cut (punchline held
  ~0.8 s) or a seamless loop back to frame 0 (the kit's loop helper).
- The joke is on the situation, never on a sign. Advice is warm: curiosity, honesty, making a plan together.
