# Month 2 boost · batch m20 · Before You Text Your Ex, episodes 1–20 (star_advice post, page 29)

Format: a thumb is about to hit SEND on a text to an ex. A giant red STOP sign drops in front of it (shake, record
scratch, freeze). THE STARS run a kind **three-question check** (three stamped cards, each ticked as the sign answers
honestly), then we see what the sign did instead, or, sometimes, the healthy version of the text that was fine to send.
The joke is on the 2 a.m. feeling, never on a sign; the advice is warm (no shame, no "win the breakup", no games).
Series bug top-left "BEFORE YOU TEXT YOUR EX". Pisces cries in every film (on screen as a friend, a bystander or the
texter). Shared series look: `promo/films/kbxe01/bxkit.js` (phone + thumb + draft, STOP drop, check cards, rooms),
imported read-only by kbxe02–20. No end card, no card transition, no logo, no season number.
Episodes 1–15 short (15–30 s), 16–20 long (60–90 s, chapter stickers, a beat every 8–10 s, payoff promised up front).
