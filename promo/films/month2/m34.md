# Month 2 boost · batch m34 · Bio Glow-Up, episodes 1–24 (star_advice trial reels, page 34)

Format: a bad dating-profile bio on a fictional profile screen (no app name, just "PROFILE"), THE STARS' red pen
slashes it, then the glow-up: an honest, specific, warm rewrite. Every bio belongs to one sign's star. A recurring
bit: ♓ Pisces in the bottom corner as the "bio judge", holding a tissue, who sobs (happy tears) when the glow-up lands.
Bug top-left: BIO GLOW-UP. Shared series look: `promo/films/kbge01/biokit.js` (profile card, red pen, strike,
rewrite, Pisces judge), imported read-only by kbge02–24, plus hookkit effects.
No end card, no card transition, no logo, no season number. The joke is on the bio, never on the sign.
Trial reels: each one makes sense to a stranger. Episodes 1–22 short (8–12 s), 23–24 long (60 s, chapter stickers,
a beat every 8–10 s, payoff promised up front). Advice is always: be specific, be honest, be kind, leave an opening.
