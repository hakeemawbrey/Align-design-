# Star Talk Then vs Now: 30 "2016 vs 2026" films (zodiac and dating)

The user asked for "30 of zodiacs/dating 2016 vs 2026 format, different years compared to now". This is the trend
where the same thing is shown "then" (an older year, with that era's grainy look) and "now" (2026, clean and current).
Most films compare 2016 with 2026; some compare 2006, 2010 or 2020 with 2026.

## Read first
Everything in **promo/films/STARTALK-TRENDS.md** applies: its "Read first" list, "What's different in this batch"
(the front-of-queue command, no season numbers, the one card cut, the trend-UI rules, lengths with the 4.4 s end card,
relatable first, Pisces crying, truth, app tie-ins, build order, report) and its "don't repeat" list. Only the
format below is new.

- **Folders:** `promo/films/yr<name>/`. **Commit:** `/tmp/claude-0/commit.sh "Star Talk Then vs Now: <title> (<len> s)" promo/films/<f>`.
- **Every film is 20 s** (15.6 s story + end card), music as listed (pick a different key per film from the list).

## The shared look (all six agents build it the same way, so the 30 films match)
- **Bug:** "THEN vs NOW", Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, top-left 56 px.
- **Big top caption:** "[Thing] in *[then year]*" for the THEN half, then "[Thing] in *2026*" for the NOW half,
  e.g. "Scorpio dating in *2016*" → "Scorpio dating in *2026*".
- **0–~7 s THEN:** a grainy, over-saturated, slightly blurry "old phone video" look: a warm color cast, a soft
  vignette, a small date stamp in the bottom-right in pixel font (e.g. "08·14·2016"), jittery framing. The era's
  gags play here (generic, never brand-specific).
- **The one card cut** sits on the switch to NOW (pass it as your middle candidate).
- **~7–13 s NOW:** clean, crisp, the current pixel-star look, with the 2026 version of the same thing. The year
  sticker flips from the old year to "2026" with a pop.
- **~13–15.6 s payoff:** both side by side (small THEN polaroid top-left, NOW big), one THE STARS box line, and the last
  line as a big headline. The turn is warm: the star grew, or the feeling stayed the same, or both.
- **The joke is gentle:** the THEN star is cringe-cute, never mocked; the NOW star isn't perfect either.
- **Era props must be generic:** a chunky older smartphone, a flip phone with number-key texting, a "selfie stick",
  a dog-ear photo filter, side-swept bangs, a burned CD labelled in marker, a webcam, a "status update" box. No real
  app, site, song, artist, show, phone or brand names, logos or UI. No real memes with owners. No pandemic or
  disaster references (for 2020, keep to "video dates" and baking at home).
