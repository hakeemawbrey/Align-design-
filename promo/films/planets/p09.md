# Planet Talk · batch p09 · Neptune (series pneptune, prefix kpne, page 40)

**Neptune:** dreamy, poetic, the romance-movie fan. Sees every love story as a film, adores a montage, and gently warns
about rose-tinted glasses. Neptune arrives like a movie: projector beams, a curtain of mist, a soft swell of strings.
In astrology Neptune rules Pisces (modern rulership) and is read as the planet of dreams, romance and illusion: framed
as a lens, never science. Astronomy (true only): Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun, it's an ice giant, and
one Neptune year lasts about 165 Earth years. No other facts.
**Running gag:** ♓ Pisces sits in Neptune's little front-row "cinema seat" bubble with a tub of popcorn and cries at
every romantic beat (happy and sad tears). Pisces cries in every film.
