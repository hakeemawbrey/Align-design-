# Planet Talk · batch p04 · Venus (page 39)

Series key `pvenus`, prefix `kpve`. 12 episodes, one per sign. Long (60–90 s): e02 Taurus, e07 Libra (Venus's home
signs). The rest are short (20–35 s). Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on star_advice (warmer,
advice-led). Rules: promo/films/PLANETS-BUILD.md + promo/films/MONTH2-BUILD.md (no end card, no card transition, no
logo or "Align" on screen, `DURATION` = story length).

**Venus (the character, via planetkit.js):** glamorous romantic, says "darling", all about love languages and
self-worth. Enters spinning *the wrong way* (true: Venus spins the opposite way to most planets) in a rose-gold blur of
glitter. Advice stays warm and healthy; astrology is a lens ("in astrology, Venus rules love and what we value").
Astronomy used, all true: Venus spins the opposite way to most planets; Venus is the brightest planet in our night sky;
a day on Venus is longer than its year; Venus is the hottest planet.

**Pisces in every film:** Pisces is Venus's starstruck assistant (a "VENUS FAN CLUB" sash; in astrology Venus is
exalted in Pisces), holding the hem of the glitter cape, and cries in every film (happy tears count). e12 is Pisces's
own episode.

**Shared look:** bug top-left "PLANET TALK · VENUS" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced). Night sky with a rose-pink
horizon, the kit's sign-meets-planet two-shot, Venus's sparkle trail. Frame 0 every film: Venus mid-entrance (mid-spin,
mid-crash, glitter mid-air) and the hook line slammed on screen; a pattern break in the first second (whip zoom,
record scratch, colour flash). Long ones get chapter stickers and a promised payoff.
