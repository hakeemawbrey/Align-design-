# Planet Talk · batch p10 · Pluto (series ppluto, prefix kppl, page 40)

**Pluto:** deep, intense, all about transformation and letting go. Small, and proud of it: wears the STILL COUNTS pin
and says "still counts" once per film. Speaks low and slow, with long, heavy pauses, then a surprisingly soft line.
Pluto arrives with menace: a slow dramatic drop, a rise over the horizon or a crash through something, with a low
boom, red-violet eclipse light and dust. Then it turns out to be tiny.
In astrology Pluto rules Scorpio (modern rulership) and is read as the planet of endings, change and letting go:
framed as a lens, never science. Astronomy (true only): Pluto is classed as a dwarf planet; it's smaller than our
Moon; it has a big heart-shaped plain; one trip round the Sun takes about 248 Earth years. No other facts.
**Pisces cries in every film:** a sobbing bystander (or the friend on the phone) in e01–e11, the star of e12.
No end card, no card transition, no "Align" on screen. Bug: PLANET TALK (top-left). Odd = star_talk (funnier), even
= star_advice (warmer, advice-led). Advice stays warm and healthy; the joke is on the situation, never on the sign.
