# Planet Talk · batch p05 · Mars (prefix kpma, episodes 01–12, page 39)

Mars is **bold, impatient, "just ask them out" energy, and means well**. A dusty red ball with a big jaw, flame-ish
brows and two tiny moons (Phobos and Deimos) orbiting his head like nervous pets. He arrives too fast every time:
crashing in, skidding, bouncing, red dust everywhere. Under the shouting, his advice is always warm and healthy
(honesty, kindness, asking clearly, hearing the answer). Each episode also lets Mars learn one small thing back.

- Look: the planetkit Mars character and the sign-meets-planet two-shot, hookkit hooks, the series bug
  PLANET TALK: MARS top-left. No end card, no card transition, no logo, "Align" never on screen.
- ♓ Pisces cries in every film (happy tears count): a bystander, a friend on the phone, or the star of ep 12.
- Astrology is a lens ("in astrology, Mars rules drive and courage"); astronomy only where true: Mars is red
  because of iron-oxide (rusty) dust; it has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos (named for fear and dread in myth);
  a day on Mars is about 40 minutes longer than ours; a Mars year is about 687 Earth days.
- Ep 01 (Aries, Mars's home sign) is long (78 s) with chapter stickers and a beat every 8–10 s. The rest are 20–35 s.
- Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on star_advice (warmer, advice-led).
