# Planet Talk · batch p07 · Saturn (page 40)

Series key `psaturn`, prefix `kpsa`. 12 episodes, one per sign. Capricorn (e10, Saturn's home sign in modern
rulership) is long (75 s); the rest are short (22–30 s). Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on
star_advice (warmer, advice-led). No end card, no card transition, no logo, "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` = story.

**Saturn's character:** strict but secretly sweet. Reading glasses, a clipboard, a pocket watch; it talks about
boundaries, patience and commitment like a tough coach, then lets the soft part slip ("…I'm proud of you. Don't make it
weird."). Built from the shared planetkit.js Saturn (rings, face, colour, voice pitch); read-only.

Shared look: `promo/films/kpsae01/sakit.js` (imported read-only by kpsae02–e12), built on planetkit.js + hookkit.js.
- Bug top-left "PLANET TALK · SATURN" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** Saturn mid-crazy-arrival (each episode a different entrance), already calling the sign out,
  the hook line slammed on screen. Pattern break in the first second (whip zoom / record scratch / colour flash).
- **Pisces cries in every film:** Pisces is Saturn's intern, clipboard in fins, and it sobs (tears level 2–3) at the
  sweet part of every episode, happy tears included. In e12 Pisces is the sign, and it cries the whole way through.
- **Astrology is a lens** ("in astrology, Saturn rules commitment…"). Astronomy only where true: Saturn has rings; the
  rings are mostly water ice; Saturn takes about 29½ years to go round the Sun.
- **Endings:** *loop* = the last 0.4 s eases back to the frame-0 arrival (kit loop helper); *hard cut* = the punchline
  holds 0.6–1.0 s, the last frame is a story frame.
- Advice stays warm and healthy (honesty, pacing, boundaries, kept promises); the joke is on the situation, never the sign.
