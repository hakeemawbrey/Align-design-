# Planet Talk · batch p02 · The Moon (page 39)

Series key `pmoon`, prefix `kpmo`. 12 episodes, one per sign. Episode 4 (Cancer, the Moon's home sign) is long
(60–90 s); the rest are short (20–35 s). Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on star_advice (warmer).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

**The Moon (character):** soft, moody, remembers every feeling, speaks in a hush (lines start lowercase, small
"shh"s, ellipses). Built from `promo/films/planetkit.js` (read-only) and `promo/films/hookkit.js` (read-only).
- Bug top-left: "PLANET TALK · THE MOON" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced, pale silver).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** the Moon mid-entrance in a crazy way (mid-crash through clouds, mid-swing down on a
  string, mid-eclipse, mid-zoom through a window), the sign caught mid-action, hook line slammed on screen.
- **Pattern break by 1 s:** whip zoom, record scratch/freeze, sudden silence (the Moon's "shh" cuts the music), or a
  colour flash. Premise lands by 3 s.
- **Pisces cries in every film:** Pisces is the Moon's little stargazing buddy, watching from a cloud with a
  telescope (a different spot each time), and sobs at the emotional peak (happy or sad tears). In e12 Pisces is the
  sign itself.
- **Truth:** astrology is a lens ("in astrology, the Moon rules feelings"). Astronomy used only where true: the Moon
  always shows Earth the same face; moonlight is reflected sunlight; the Moon has craters; the Moon's pull drives the
  tides; the new moon is the phase we barely see; the Moon has orbited Earth for billions of years; phases repeat
  about every month.
- Advice warm and healthy; the joke is on the situation, never the sign.
