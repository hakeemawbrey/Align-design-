# Planet Talk · batch p03 · Mercury (series `pmercury`, prefix `kpme`, page 39)

Mercury: the fast talker who texts in bursts, the overthinker's coach. Home signs (long, 60–90 s): Gemini (e03) and
Virgo (e06). Everything else short (20–35 s). Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even on star_advice (warmer).
Rules: promo/films/PLANETS-BUILD.md + promo/films/MONTH2-BUILD.md. No end card, no card transition, no logo, no
"Align" on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

Shared look: Mercury from `promo/films/planetkit.js` (read-only), hooks from `promo/films/hookkit.js` (read-only),
signs from startalk.js. Any Mercury-batch extras live in `promo/films/kpmee01/mercurykit.js` (imported by e02–e12).
- Bug top-left "PLANET TALK · MERCURY" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** Mercury already mid-arrival at speed (skid, ricochet, burst through something), speed
  lines and smoke, the sign in shot reacting, the hook line slammed on screen. Pattern break inside the first second
  (whip zoom / record scratch / glitch / sudden silence).
- **Mercury's voice:** text bubbles in fast bursts (3–4 bubbles stacking quickly), fast talk cues, high pitch.
- **Pisces cries in every film** (happy or sad tears), as the sign itself, a friend, a contact or a bystander.
- Astrology framed as a lens ("in astrology, Mercury rules how we talk"). Astronomy only where true: Mercury is the
  closest planet to the Sun; a Mercury year is about 88 Earth days.
- Advice is warm and honest; the joke is on the situation, never the sign.
