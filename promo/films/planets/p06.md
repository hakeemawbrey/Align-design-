# Planet Talk · batch p06 · Jupiter (page 40)

Series key `pjupiter`, prefix `kpju`. 12 episodes, one per sign. Ep 9 (Sagittarius, Jupiter's home sign) is long
(60–90 s); the rest are short (20–35 s). Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on star_advice (warmer,
advice-led). No end card, no card transition, no logo, "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

**Jupiter's character:** big generous uncle energy, everything is an adventure. Booming, warm, calls everyone
"kiddo", too big for every frame, always arrives mid-crash. Jupiter's area (astrology as a lens): luck, growth,
generosity, adventure, saying yes to life. Built on promo/films/planetkit.js (character, two-shot, entrance) and
promo/films/hookkit.js (hooks), both read-only. Shared Jupiter extras (props, Pisces cameo) go in
`promo/films/kpjue01/jupkit.js`, imported read-only by e02–e12.
- Bug top-left "PLANET TALK · JUPITER" (Inter 800 small caps, letter-spaced).
- **Frame 0 (every film):** Jupiter mid-arrival, mid-crash, too big for the frame, plus the hook line already slammed.
  Pattern break inside the first second (whip zoom, shake, scratch-freeze or colour flash).
- **Pisces cries in every film:** a cameo with visible tears (happy tears count) in e01–e11; the whole of e12.
- **Astronomy used (all true):** Jupiter is the biggest planet; it has dozens of moons; it takes about 12 years to go
  round the Sun (so, in astrology, it spends about a year in each sign). Astrology framed as a lens ("in astrology,
  Jupiter rules…"). Modern rulership: Jupiter rules Sagittarius.
