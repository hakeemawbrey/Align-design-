# Planet Talk · batch p08 · Uranus (page 40)

Series key `puranus`, prefix `kpur`. Home sign Aquarius (ep 11) is long (60–90 s); the rest are short (20–35 s).
Odd episodes post on star_talk (funnier), even ones on star_advice (warmer, advice-led).
No end card, no card transition, no logo, the word "Align" never on screen. `DURATION` = story length.

**Uranus, the character:** chaotic genius. A cyan-teal ice giant that rolls in **on its side** (true: Uranus spins
tipped over on its side), crackling with ideas, wild hair-like streaks of static, goggles pushed up. Catchphrase
energy: "What if you did the *unexpected*?" The twist is always warm and healthy: the "unexpected" move is honesty,
kindness or trying something new, never a game. Astrology framed as a lens ("in astrology, Uranus rules sudden
change and Aquarius"). Built on promo/films/planetkit.js (character, two-shot, entrance hook) and
promo/films/hookkit.js (whip zoom, scratch/freeze, glitch, notification, loading bar, chapter sticker, loop-back).
Batch-local helpers live in `promo/films/kpure01/urkit.js`.

- **Frame 0 (every film):** Uranus mid-crash-landing in the sign's scene (already moving, things mid-"went flying"),
  static sparks, the hook line slammed on screen. Pattern break in the first second (glitch flash / whip zoom /
  scratch-freeze). Premise lands by 3 s.
- **Pisces cries in every film** (a cameo in the background or a reaction cut; happy or sad tears).
