Sagittarius falls for minds first. Bring a good argument and a passport.

Jupiter rules philosophy, travel and big ideas. That is the Sagittarius love language.

They are drawn to people who teach them something. Who can debate. Who have stories from somewhere else. A Sagittarius will forgive a messy flat long before they forgive a dull conversation.

Looks might start the conversation. Curiosity keeps it going.

If you want to keep a Sagittarius, do not settle into routine. Keep learning, keep exploring. Bring them along.
