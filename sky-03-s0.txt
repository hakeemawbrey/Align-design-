Venus tells you what you find beautiful. Mars tells you what you'll chase.

Two planets, two kinds of wanting.

Venus is attraction. Taste. What makes you soft. The kind of love you want to receive. Venus is the meal you would cook for someone, and the one you hope they would cook for you.

Mars is drive. Pursuit. What makes you move. How you go after what you want, and how you fight.

When they disagree, you might fall for one type and chase another. Knowing both is how you stop being surprised by yourself.
