The stars don't decide who you love. They describe how you'll do it.

Astrology is not a cage. Your chart does not choose your partner, or guarantee anything.

What it does is describe. How you open up. What makes you feel safe. Where you tend to push, and where you tend to hide.

Think of it as a weather report for your heart, not a contract.

The choices are still yours. The stars just help you see the patterns you bring to them.
