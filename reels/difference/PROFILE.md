# The page: Align, what's the difference

The reels in this folder can post from the main Align account or a page of
their own. The format grows by shares ("send this to the friend who…"), not
comments, so there's no comment-to-DM funnel to set up. Every caption ends on
the free chart and the link in bio.

## Handle and name

| | Pick | Backups |

| Name field | `What's the difference? · Love & Astrology` | `Align · Love words, explained` |

at the top of `render.mjs` and re-render.
