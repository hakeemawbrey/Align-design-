# Audio sources

All files come from OpenGameArt.org and every one is licensed **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)**:
commercial use is allowed, no attribution is required, and modification is allowed.
I checked the licence on each OGA source page on 2026-11-09. No CC-BY material is used.

All files were converted to 48 kHz / 16-bit / stereo WAV and trimmed of leading silence.
SFX are peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, and tails are trimmed with a short fade.
`music.wav` is the whole track, with its level reduced by 0.7 dB to get -1 dBFS peak headroom.
