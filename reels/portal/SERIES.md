## Format and cadence

Each reel follows the reference's shape, in Align's own dot-matrix art. A dated
hook comes first ("…is Tuesday", "…opens in two days"), with "here's what to do"
for love. Then two or three lines of real sky or number logic, and three steps,
each a single verb, lit on the tracker at the top as each one starts. One line
says "the Align app" and ties the ritual to something the app really does:
the sky before the face, release leaving your deck, talk cards hidden until
you've both answered, dealbreakers on your card, a veiled card, a new deck every
night, your Moon sign read from your birth time, a match only when it's mutual.
The reel ends on the three verbs stacked as words, then the hook object again
with the **+ FOLLOW** pill. It runs about 50–58 seconds, at 120–140 spoken words.

Post each reel **two days before its date**, so the hook ("in two days", "is
Tuesday") is literally true on the day people see it. That gives roughly one
portal a week through the holidays. The gaps are at Nov 13–24 and Dec 12–21,
which leaves room for the affirm and signs reels between. Pin 01 while it's
live, and swap the pin to the next portal once it's posted. The renderer adds the follow line to every caption, so each episode points to the next.
