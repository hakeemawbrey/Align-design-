# PS2 cutscene reels: references

Notes on accounts that make short reels in a 2002-era console cutscene
style. These are text notes only: no downloaded footage, stills or audio are
kept in this repo. We borrow the **structure** of the joke, never their
characters, footage, lines or audio (see `reels/SPEC.md`).

## The shared format

- **Look:** low-poly characters, blurry textures, flat lighting, a slightly
  over-sharpened render. It reads as a PS2 cutscene. Black-and-white is used
  for the "cool" or "after" shot.
- **Length:** 10 to 25 seconds, vertical.
- **Shape:** a straight-faced dramatic setup, a short punchline, then the hero
  shot in the product. The product is the punchline's payoff, never pitched.
- **Text:** chunky white all-caps captions (unemployed_corp) or yellow
  game-style subtitles along the bottom (demitrivornov0). Few words per beat.
- **Caption:** one deadpan line, lowercase or sentence case.

## @unemployed_corp: "work kills slowly."

Reel `DfPlLIUgw50`, posted 2026-11-02. About 25.8k likes and 205 comments.
About 23 seconds. Sells a "Full-Time Unemployed" shirt.

| Time | Shot | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Office morgue. A body under a sheet, a work ID badge hanging from the hand | DETECTIVE WHAT KILLED |
| 3–5s | The detective closes his eyes, solemn | 9 TO 5 |
| 6–8s | The detective and the coroner look on, deadpan | ANOTHER VICTIM |
| 9–14s | Black and white: the hero, alive, in the shirt and shades, walks out of the station | none |
| 15–23s | Product shot of the shirt with the website | the URL |

Why it works: crime-scene gravity applied to an everyday thing, a punchline
of three words or fewer, then the hero who avoided that fate.

and every beat is visual.

## How Align uses it

A series with two recurring characters, **Him** and **Her**, a single man
and a single woman. Both are original to Align. The detective and other
side characters stay the same across episodes.

**Format A, the crime scene (from unemployed_corp):**
"Detective, what killed him?" "He didn't align." "Another victim." Then the
black-and-white shot of the two of them, aligned, walking out together,
then the Align end card at 11:11.

| Victim | Cause of death | Align idea in the after shot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Her | An eight-month talking stage | it has to be mutual |
| Him | She never wanted kids. He never asked. | dealbreakers up front |
| Him | He swiped on a face | read the sky before the face |
| Her | A situationship | say the honest thing |
| Him | She ghosted him (a literal ghost) | release, don't haunt |
| Her | "It wasn't mutual." | it has to be mutual |
| Him | He said "I'm fine." | say the honest thing |

**Format B, the comeback (from demitrivornov0):** someone taunts Him or Her
about being single ("Still single? Shame no one swipes on you."). A silent
stare, then the reveal: their match walking up, or an "It's mutual" screen.
The comeback line is an Align line.

Voice rules from `reels/SPEC.md` still apply. Never say "compatible" or
"compatibility"; it's always "align".
