Rest is not a reward for finishing. It's part of the work.

You only let yourself rest when everything is done. And everything is never done.

So you run on empty and call it discipline. You treat tiredness like a character flaw.

But nothing in nature works that way. The moon has dark nights. Fields lie fallow. Tides go out.

Rest is not falling behind. It is how you stay in the race. Take it before you have earned it. That is the point.
