They didn't lose interest. They showed you their attention span.

It felt like it changed overnight. The good morning texts slowed. The plans got vague.

But maybe nothing changed. Maybe this is who they always were, and the first few weeks were the exception.

Some people love the beginning. The chase, the newness, the version of themselves they get to be in it. When it becomes ordinary, they drift.

That is information, not a verdict on you. You were not too much or not enough. You just met someone whose interest has a shelf life. Keep looking for the long-life kind.
