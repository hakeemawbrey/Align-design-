Mercury retrograde didn't make you text them. That was you.

It has become the universal excuse. The text to the ex. The misunderstanding. The rash decision at midnight.

Astrologically, Mercury retrograde is a time for review. Re-reading, re-thinking, revisiting. That can bring old things up. Old friends text, exes resurface, half-finished conversations come back for a second draft.

But the stars do not hold your phone.

If an old feeling surfaces, notice it. Just do not hand your choices over to a planet. Review is not the same as relapse.
