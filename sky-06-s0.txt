An eclipse doesn't end things. It shows you what was already over.

Eclipse seasons have a reputation. Breakups, sudden moves, endings that come out of nowhere.

But look closer and very little comes out of nowhere. Eclipses tend to light up what was already shifting. The relationship that had been fading. The job you had already left in your head.

They are not the cause. They are the reveal.

If something leaves in eclipse season, it was probably already on its way out.
