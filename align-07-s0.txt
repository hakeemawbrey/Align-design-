Rejection is just redirection with bad manners.

It stings. The no, the silence, the person who chose someone else.

But look back at your life. How many rejections turned out to be the reason you ended up somewhere better?

The job you did not get. The person who did not stay. Both cleared the path for something that fit.

It is allowed to hurt. It is also allowed to be the best thing that never happened to you.
