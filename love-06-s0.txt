The right one won't feel like a rescue. It'll feel like a rest.

Some love arrives like an emergency. Everything is urgent. You are saved, then wrecked, then saved again.

It is thrilling, and it is tiring, and it teaches you that love is supposed to feel like adrenaline.

The right one might feel almost boring at first. No drama to solve. No test to pass. Just someone who texts back and means it.

If your nervous system has learned chaos as chemistry, peace can feel unfamiliar. Unfamiliar is not the same as wrong. Give calm a chance to become home.
