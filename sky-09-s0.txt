Saturn returns don't ruin your life. They audit it.

Around 29, something shifts. The career that looked fine starts to feel wrong. The relationship you assumed was forever gets questioned.

That is the Saturn return. The planet of structure coming back to where it was when you were born.

It is not punishment. It is an inspection. What is real stays. What is not starts to crack.

It is uncomfortable. It is also how you end up building a life that actually holds.
