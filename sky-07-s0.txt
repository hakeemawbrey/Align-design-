Your rising sign is how strangers meet you. Your chart is how lovers do.

The rising sign is the first impression. The way you walk in, the vibe people pick up before you speak.

It is useful, but it is the front door. It tells people how to approach you, not who they will find once they are inside.

The rest of the chart is the house. The moon in the kitchen. Venus in the bedroom. Saturn in the locked room you do not show anyone.

Strangers meet the door. The right person wants the whole tour.
