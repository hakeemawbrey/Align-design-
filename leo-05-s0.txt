Leo loves like the sun shines. Generously, daily, and a little bit at you.

The sun does not shine selectively. It warms everything.

Leo loves the same way. Friends, family, partners, strangers who need a laugh. Their generosity is a big part of who they are. They are the friend who pays for dinner and remembers everyone's birthday, loudly.

And yes, they like you to notice the warmth. To turn your face toward it.

It is a fair trade. Few signs give so much light. All they ask is that you enjoy it out loud.
