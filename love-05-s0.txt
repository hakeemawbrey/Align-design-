Being wanted and being chosen are different things. Learn to feel the difference.

Being wanted is a text at midnight. Being chosen is a plan for Saturday.

Being wanted feels like heat. Being chosen feels like calm. One happens to you when it suits them. The other happens on purpose, in daylight, in front of their friends.

A lot of people spend years confusing the two, because wanted is easier to get and feels incredible for an evening.

You deserve both. But if you can only have one, take the one that still shows up when the novelty wears off.
