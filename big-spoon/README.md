# Big Spoon Concepts — Marketing Build: Scope & Audit

18-slide proposal deck. Open `index.html` in a browser.

- Navigate: arrow keys, space, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End, or click (left third goes back). `#n` in the URL jumps to slide n.
- PDF: `big-spoon-marketing-build.pdf` (one slide per page). Re-export with the browser's Print → Save as PDF, or headless Chromium.
- Art: `assets/art/` — 23 public-domain (CC0) works from The Met and Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access, each used once, chosen in the art picker (`art-candidates.json`). Color and contrast lightly lifted. Full credits on the last slide.
- Logos: drop files into `assets/logos/`; until then the concept names are set in type.
