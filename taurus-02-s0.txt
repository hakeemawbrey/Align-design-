A Taurus won't rush you. They also won't move once they've decided.

Taurus takes its time. They watch. They test the ground. They are not jumping into anything.

But once a Taurus has decided, the decision is made. Fixed signs commit in concrete.

That is wonderful if they have decided on you. Less so if you are hoping they will change their mind about something.

With Taurus, be patient at the beginning. The steadiness you get at the end is worth it.
