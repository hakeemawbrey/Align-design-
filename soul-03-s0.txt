The same lesson keeps finding you in different outfits.

A new boss who talks over you. A new partner who does too. A friend who only calls when they need something.

The faces change. The feeling does not. That sinking, familiar sense of having been here before.

That is not the universe punishing you. It is the lesson coming back because it was not finished.

Pay attention to repetition. The moment you respond differently, the outfit stops showing up.

Save this for the night you need it. Then come align. Link in bio.
