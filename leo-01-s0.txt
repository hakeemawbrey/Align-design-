Leo doesn't need you to be impressed. They need you to be glad they're here.

Leo is fixed fire, ruled by the sun. Warm, generous, built to shine.

People assume Leo wants applause. Sometimes. But underneath it is something simpler. The sun does not need applause to shine. It just likes to be noticed warming things up.

They want to feel that their presence matters. That the room is better because they walked in. That you are happy they chose you.

Tell them. Not with flattery, but with honesty. A Leo who feels welcome will light up your whole life.
