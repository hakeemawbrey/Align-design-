Alignment feels less like winning and more like finally exhaling.

We expect the right thing to feel like a victory. Fireworks. Triumph.

Often it is much quieter. Your shoulders drop. Your jaw unclenches. You realise you have stopped bracing. Your phone stops feeling like a slot machine. Your evenings stop revolving around a reply.

That is the feeling. Not the high, but the rest.

If you are waiting to feel like you won, you may miss it. Pay attention to when you finally breathe out.

Stop forcing it. Come align. Link in bio.
