Full moons don't make you emotional. They make you honest.

Something about a full moon brings it all to the surface. The feeling you were avoiding. The thing you meant to say.

It is a moment of illumination. Everything that was half hidden gets a little more light. Astrologically, it is the sun and moon facing each other across the sky, and nothing stays hidden in that much light.

That can feel like chaos. It is usually just truth arriving.

Use it. Say the thing. Write it down. Let go of what the light shows you is finished.
