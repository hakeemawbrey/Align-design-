# January 2027 sky news plan

One post a day, 31 posts. Every sky fact is from `news/data/sky.json` / `data/SKY.md` and listed in each post's `facts`; `node news/src/check.mjs news/posts/2027-01` passes 31/31. Times are UTC, as in sky.json.

Notes:
- Mars turns direct after the data range ends. A scratch run of `node news/src/sky.mjs 2027-01-01 2027-05-01` (outside the repo) gives the direct station on 2027-04-01 at 20.9° leo, but the checker can't verify it against data/sky.json, so posts say "into spring" and the date appears nowhere.
- Mars backs into leo on feb 21 (in data): used on jan 10 and jan 14.
- Feb 6 eclipse: the only location fact used is the greatest-eclipse point in sky.json (about 31°s, 48°w, the south atlantic). Posts point to a local eclipse map instead of claiming who can see it.
- Mercury enters pisces 01:27 utc jan 31 (evening of jan 30 in new york). It's called jan 31 / 01:27 utc.
- Sky-note topics were picked to avoid dec/feb repeats (ophiuchus, "retrograde means backwards", full moon crazy, blood moon): jan does mars-retrograde dating doom, mercury in aquarius "unavailable" and eclipse karmic-debt fear-bait.
- Cover mix: 10 hero, 2 breaking (mars station jan 10, eclipse t-7 jan 30), 4 split, 15 cover. Tables inside jan 19 and jan 26; story insets on jan 1, 11, 17, 22, 26, 29, 31.
