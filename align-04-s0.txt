When it's aligned, you stop asking your friends what it means.

Remember the group chats. Screenshots. What does this mean? Is this a good sign? Should I wait?

When something is right, those conversations dry up. Not because you stopped caring, but because there is nothing to decode.

They said they would call, and they called. They said they liked you, and they acted like it.

Aligned love is legible. If you need a translator, you might be in the wrong language.
