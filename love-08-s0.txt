Stop calling it bad luck in love. It's a pattern with your name on it.

Different faces, same story. The one who needs saving. The one who cannot commit. The one who makes you prove it.

It is easy to call that bad luck. Luck is random. This is not random. This is a pattern, and patterns have a source.

Ask the honest question. What do all of them have in common, apart from you? What did you overlook, excuse, or find exciting each time?

That is not blame. It is power. A pattern you can see is a pattern you can stop choosing.
