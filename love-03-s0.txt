Chemistry is how fast it starts. Alignment is why it lasts.

Chemistry is loud. It is the spark, the pull, the three-hour first date that felt like ten minutes.

Alignment is quieter. It is wanting the same kind of Sunday. Fighting fair. Meaning the same thing when you both say forever.

Plenty of people have chemistry with the wrong person. It is the most convincing feeling in the world, and it tells you almost nothing about year three.

You do not have to choose between them. But if you only ever chase the spark, you will keep starting fires and wondering why nothing gets built.
