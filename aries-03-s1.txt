Aries needs a partner who can keep up, not one who keeps score.

Aries moves fast. Ideas, plans, apologies. They will forget the argument by lunch and expect you to as well.

What they cannot handle is a ledger. Old mistakes brought back months later. Points tallied. To an Aries, a fight is weather. It blows through, and then the sun comes out again.

They want someone who can match their pace and their honesty. Who can say it straight and move on.

Keep up, and you will never be bored. Keep score, and you will lose them.
