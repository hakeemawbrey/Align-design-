Gemini needs someone they can talk to at 3am about nothing and everything.

For a Gemini, conversation is intimacy. Late night talks. Voice notes on the walk home. A text thread that never quite ends.

They want a partner who is curious, quick, and up for the tangent. Someone who can follow a story into three side stories and still remember where it started.

The silent type might look mysterious at first. For a Gemini, mystery gets old fast.

They want the person who answers at 3am with an opinion. That is their kind of romantic.
