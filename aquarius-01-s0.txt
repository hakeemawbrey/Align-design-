Aquarius loves you in their own way. You might need the instruction manual.

Aquarius is fixed air, ruled by Saturn and, in modern astrology, Uranus. Original, independent, unpredictable.

They do not always love in the expected ways. Less flowers, more sending you an article they knew you would like. Less cuddling, more defending your weird idea in public. They will remember the thing you said you cared about and quietly fight for it.

It is still love. It just needs translating.

Learn their language. It is one of the most loyal dialects in the zodiac.
