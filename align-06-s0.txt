Divine timing is real. So is you hiding behind it.

Sometimes the timing really is not right. Some doors open later. Some people arrive when you are ready.

And sometimes we use timing as a place to hide. It will happen when it is meant to, we say, while doing nothing to let it.

Trust the timing and still do your part. Go to the thing. Send the message. Make the move.

The universe does the scheduling. You still have to show up for the appointment.

Stop forcing it. Come align. Link in bio.
