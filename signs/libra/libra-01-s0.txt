Libra will say anywhere is fine. Anywhere is not fine. Ask again.

Libra is cardinal air, ruled by Venus. Harmony matters to them deeply, sometimes more than their own dinner order.

So they compromise. They go along. They pick whatever restaurant you like. They say anywhere is fine, then quietly hate the restaurant all evening.

But every unspoken preference adds up. Eventually the peace they kept starts to feel like a prison.

If you love a Libra, ask what they want. Then wait, properly, for the answer. Having a preference is not starting a fight.
