Libra falls in love with beauty. They stay for fairness.

Venus gives Libra an eye for beauty. A gorgeous face, a lovely room, an elegant gesture.

That might be what draws them in. But it is not what keeps them. A pretty face can get a first date. Only a fair one gets a second year.

What keeps a Libra is fairness. A relationship where both people give. Where arguments are resolved, not won.

The scales are their symbol for a reason. Balance is their love language.
