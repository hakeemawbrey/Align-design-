An Aquarius won't be jealous. They'll just quietly expect you to be as free as they are.

Aquarius dislikes possessiveness. They will not track your location or demand to know where you were.

They give freedom easily. And they expect the same. To them, love is a choice made freely every day, not a lock on the door.

That can feel like detachment to some signs. It is actually trust.

If you want an Aquarius, do not cage them. Let them come and go. They always come back to the people who let them be themselves.
