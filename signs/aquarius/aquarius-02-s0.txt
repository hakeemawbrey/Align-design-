Aquarius is a friend first. If they're not your friend, they're not your person.

For Aquarius, friendship is the foundation of everything. Romance without it feels hollow to them.

They want someone they can talk to for hours. Laugh with. Disagree with. Plan the future of the world with. Romance is the extra layer. Friendship is the building it sits on, and they check the foundations first.

If you skip the friendship and go straight to romance, they may drift.

Be their friend. Truly. The rest tends to follow.
