Aquarius will debate the future of humanity and dodge one question about their feelings.

Aquarius values independence. Space to think. Room to be themselves.

But sometimes that space becomes a hiding place. When things get emotional, they step back. Analyse instead of feel. They will talk about systems, ideas, anything but the one question you actually asked them.

It can look like calm. Often it is protection.

Aquarius, staying in the room when it gets personal is not losing yourself. Answer the one question. It is how someone gets to love the real you.
