Venus rules Taurus. They don't love loudly. They love in thread count.

Taurus is Venus at her most earthly. Pleasure, comfort, beauty you can touch.

They show love through the senses. The good sheets. The meal they spent all afternoon making. A hand on your back when you walk past.

They will not always say it in words. They will make your life softer.

If you are dating a Taurus, look at your surroundings. Chances are, you are being loved in the details.
