Taurus calls it patience. Sometimes it's just refusing to leave a bad thing.

The strength of Taurus is staying power. They do not quit when it gets hard.

But that strength has a shadow. Sometimes they stay long after something has stopped being good, simply because leaving feels harder than enduring. Fixed earth does not like to be moved, even by its own better judgment.

Patience is a virtue. Stubbornness dressed as patience is not.

If you are a Taurus, ask yourself honestly. Am I waiting for this to improve, or just afraid to let go?
