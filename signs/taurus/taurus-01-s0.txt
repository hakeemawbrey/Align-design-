Taurus remembers how you take your coffee three years after the breakup.

Taurus is fixed earth, ruled by Venus. They notice details through the senses. Tastes, textures, the song that was playing.

When a Taurus loves you, they store it. The way you laugh. Your favourite dinner. How you like your coffee.

And that does not just disappear when it ends.

It can make them slow to move on. It also makes them one of the most devoted partners in the zodiac. They do not just love you. They learn you.
