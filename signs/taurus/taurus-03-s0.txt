Taurus doesn't want fireworks. They want you to show up on Sunday.

Some signs want grand gestures. Taurus wants consistency.

A standing date. A meal you cook together. Knowing you will be there without having to ask. Your name in their calendar, in pen, every week, without being reminded.

To a Taurus, reliability is romance. Showing up is the love letter.

If you want to win one over, skip the surprise trip. Be the person they can count on, every week, for the ordinary things. That is the whole secret.
