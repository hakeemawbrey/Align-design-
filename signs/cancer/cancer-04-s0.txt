Cancer's loyalty has no expiry date. Neither does their memory.

When a Cancer commits, they commit for life. Friends from childhood. Family they will defend to anyone.

That loyalty is fierce. And it comes with a memory to match. They can tell you what you wore on your first date and what you said that hurt in year two.

They remember every kindness. They also remember every hurt.

If you love a Cancer, be careful with them. They will hold onto the good moments for years. Give them plenty.
