Behind every loud Leo is someone who just wants to hear they did well.

Leo performs confidence. And often it is real. But there is a tender spot underneath.

They want to know they are doing well. That their effort was noticed. That the risk they took was worth it. Under the big personality is a child holding up a drawing, waiting for someone to look.

They will not always ask. Asking feels like admitting doubt.

So tell them, unprompted. You did well. I'm proud of you. Watch how quickly the sun comes out.
