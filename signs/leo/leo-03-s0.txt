Leo will forgive almost anything except being ignored.

Fights a Leo can handle. Disagreements, even mistakes.

What cuts deep is indifference. Being overlooked. Being treated like background. Being treated like the room would have been exactly the same without them.

The sun cannot stand being hidden behind a cloud.

If you love a Leo, notice them. Ask about their day. Watch their thing. React to their news. Attention is not vanity to them. It is how they know they are loved.
