A Leo in love makes a Tuesday feel like an opening night.

Leo does not do half-hearted. When they love you, they love you out loud.

The surprise dinner. The compliment in front of friends. The way they make an ordinary evening feel like an event. They plan the birthday months ahead and remember the small thing you said you wanted.

That generosity is real. They want you to feel special because they know how good it feels.

If you are with a Leo, let yourself be celebrated. And remember to celebrate them back.
