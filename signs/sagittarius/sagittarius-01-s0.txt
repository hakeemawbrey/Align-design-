Sagittarius isn't scared of commitment. They're scared of a smaller life.

Sagittarius is mutable fire, ruled by Jupiter, the planet of expansion.

They get labelled as commitment-phobic. The truth is more specific. They fear any love that makes their world shrink. Less freedom, less laughter, less room for the person they are still becoming.

Fewer adventures, fewer ideas, fewer horizons.

The partner who keeps a Sagittarius is the one who makes life bigger. Book the trip together. Learn something new together. Then watch how happily they stay.
