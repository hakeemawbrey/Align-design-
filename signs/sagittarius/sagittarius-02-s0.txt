A Sagittarius will tell you the truth even when you were hoping for a nice lie.

Honesty is the archer's arrow. It flies straight, sometimes faster than intended.

A Sagittarius will tell you if they think you are wrong. If the outfit is not working. If the plan has holes. The archer does not aim to wound. They just never learned to bend the arrow.

It can sting. But you will never have to wonder what they really think.

In a world full of polite evasions, that is a gift. Let it land. They mean it kindly.
