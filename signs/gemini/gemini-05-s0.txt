A Gemini will explain their feelings brilliantly. Feeling them is the harder part.

Mercury gives Gemini a gift for words. They can describe an emotion perfectly. Analyse it. Make a joke about it.

What is harder is sitting with it. A Gemini can talk about a feeling for an hour and still not have let themselves feel it.

They might answer a feeling with a fact, or change the subject when it gets too close.

If you love a Gemini, give them room to talk it through. Then gently ask the second question. The one about how it actually feels.
