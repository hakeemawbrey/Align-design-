Bore a Gemini once and they'll forgive you. Bore them twice and they're typing.

Gemini needs stimulation the way some signs need security. Conversation, new ideas, a reason to keep coming back.

They will forgive a lot. A bad date. A forgotten plan. But a relationship that has stopped surprising them? That is hard.

It is not shallowness. Their mind is a fire that needs fuel.

Ask them a question you have never asked. Take them somewhere new. Keep the conversation alive, and they will stay.
