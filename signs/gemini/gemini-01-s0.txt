Gemini isn't two-faced. They just have more than one thing to say to you.

Gemini is mutable air, ruled by Mercury. Their minds move fast and in several directions at once.

That gets misread as inconsistency. It is really curiosity. They are thinking about you from five angles at the same time.

The trick is to enjoy the range rather than try to pin it down.

You will never run out of things to talk about. And that, to a Gemini, is the deepest kind of love.
