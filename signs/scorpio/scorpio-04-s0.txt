Scorpio would rather have one real thing than ten easy ones.

Casual is not really in the Scorpio vocabulary.

They are not interested in surface level. Small talk bores them. Half-hearted connection frustrates them. They would rather have one conversation that changes them than a hundred that pass the time.

They want depth. Truth. Something that feels like it matters.

That makes them choosy. It also makes them one of the most devoted partners in the zodiac. If a Scorpio is giving you their time, you are already one of the real ones.
