Scorpio tests you because they've been fooled before. Pass, and they're yours.

Scorpio has a sixth sense for lies. Partly intuition. Partly experience.

So they test people, sometimes without meaning to. A small push to see if you will stay. A question to see if you are honest. They are not trying to catch you out. They are trying to stop being caught out themselves.

It can feel unfair. It comes from having been hurt.

Be consistent. Be truthful. Do not take the tests personally. Scorpio is looking for someone who will still be there after the storm.
