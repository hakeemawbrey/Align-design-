Under the Scorpio stare is someone hoping you won't leave once you see everything.

Scorpio has a reputation for intensity. Piercing eyes, guarded heart, mystery.

But what is underneath is surprisingly tender. A fear that if someone truly saw them, all of them, they would leave. So they reveal themselves slowly, a room at a time, watching how you react.

That is why they hide. Why they test. Why they hold back.

If you love a Scorpio, stay through the reveal. Let them see that the truth about them is not a dealbreaker.
