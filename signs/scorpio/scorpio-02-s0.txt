A Scorpio can forgive you. They just won't pretend it didn't happen.

There is a myth that Scorpio never forgives. Not quite true.

They can forgive. But they do not erase. The memory stays, and with it, the lesson. Water remembers the shape of every stone that was ever thrown into it.

That is not holding a grudge. It is refusing to be naive.

If you have hurt a Scorpio and they have stayed, take it seriously. You have been given a second chance by someone who rarely gives them.
