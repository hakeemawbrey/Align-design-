Aries doesn't play hard to get. If they like you, you'll know by Thursday.

There is no slow reveal, no strategic silence. If an Aries is interested, they will text first, plan the date, and tell you they had a good time before you have got home.

It can feel like a lot. It is actually rare.

Most people make you guess. Aries hands you the answer. Enjoy the clarity while everyone else is still decoding.
