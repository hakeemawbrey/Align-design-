A Virgo notices everything. The trick is letting you see that they adore it.

Virgo sees the details. Your new haircut. The slight change in your mood. The typo in your text.

That attention is a gift. But Virgo sometimes only voices the critical part. The praise stays in their head and only the corrections make it out loud.

The truth is, most of what they notice, they love. Your habits. Your little rituals. The things nobody else sees.

Virgo, say those things too. Let people know your attention comes from affection, not inspection.
