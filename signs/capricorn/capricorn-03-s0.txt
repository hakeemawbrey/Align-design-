Capricorn has a five-year plan and no idea how they feel today. Ask them.

Saturn makes Capricorn disciplined. Work first. Five-year plan second. Feelings when there is time.

The problem is that there is never quite enough time. Next quarter. After the deadline. Once things calm down. Things rarely calm down.

So emotions pile up in a waiting room. And Capricorn carries on like everything is fine.

If you love one, ask how they really are. Then wait. They might not answer straight away. But they will remember you asked.
