A Capricorn will show up in a crisis. Let them show up when it's easy, too.

Capricorn is the person you call when things fall apart. Calm. Practical. Already making a plan.

But they often forget that love is not only for emergencies. They know how to be needed. Being wanted for no reason makes them a little nervous.

They can be so busy being useful that they never let themselves just be wanted.

If you love a Capricorn, invite them to do nothing. A slow dinner. A lazy morning. Remind them that they matter even when nothing needs fixing.
