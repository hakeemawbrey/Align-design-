Aquarius doesn't care what people think. They care a lot about what you think.

The water bearer is famously unbothered. Trends, opinions, what the neighbours say.

But there is a short list of people whose opinions matter deeply. If you are on it, you have real influence. They will shrug off a stranger's criticism and lie awake over one honest word from you.

Aquarius just will not always show how much your view means.

Be kind with that power. Tell them what you admire. They will act like it is nothing. They will remember it forever.
