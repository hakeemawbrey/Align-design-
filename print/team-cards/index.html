Printing

  1. Print double-sided at 100% / actual size, never “fit to page”, on 100 lb cover cardstock or heavier, matte or silk.
  2. Cut on the crop marks. Each card has its own marks with a strip of extra artwork between neighbours, so make two cuts between cards.
  3. Cut cards are 2.5 × 3.5 in, standard trading-card size: they fit penny sleeves and 3 × 4 in, 35pt top-loaders. Round the corners with a 1/8 in corner punch if you like.
