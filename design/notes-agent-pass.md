# Parallel pass — what landed

Four agents ran concurrently over disjoint screen sets. One (the deck loop)
reported in full; its notes are in `notes-deck.md`. The other three were killed
by a shared account rate limit before they could write theirs, so **this file
records what I verified on screen afterwards**, not what those agents reported.
Their own reasoning is lost.

## Verified on screen

### Onboarding
- **O-05 Sign Reveal now reads `Sun in Gemini`.** The persona contradiction is
  resolved — it previously revealed Libra while `G-05 You` established the
  account as *Hakeem, 32 — Gemini · Air · Sagittarius moon*. The constellation
  and prose were rebuilt for Gemini.
- **O-02 Arrival carries the chakra column** on its focal body.
- **O-03 Birth Data now uses wheel pickers** (month / day / year), which is
  Stardust's input pattern and a large improvement on whatever preceded it.
- **O-13 Photo Upload** uses six **card-shaped** photo slots, one filled and
  labelled `CARD FACE`. The deck motif carried into onboarding without being
  asked for, which is the right instinct: your photo becomes a card face.
- **O-01 Splash** carries the vesica figure.

### Matches
Restructured from an undifferentiated 2x3 grid into **three labelled sections**:
`YOUR TURN · 2`, `WAITING ON THEM · 2`, `GOING QUIET · 2`, with a summary line
*"9 aligned · 2 waiting on you · 2 going quiet"* and per-tile status chips
(`Veil lifting`, `Your turn`, `2 days left`, `18 hours`). This was the biggest
open UX gap in that group — the grid previously communicated no state at all.

### Alignment sheet
**The chakra-column device is built.** Two columns of seven centres, one per
person, lit where they agree. This was the strongest unclaimed idea in the
system and it now exists.

### Paywall
Timeline (`Today` / `This week` / `In 7 days`), two plan cards (Founding $10
once-forever vs Monthly $6.99), a pale cream `7 days free` badge, holo CTA
`Claim founding member`. **No red, no urgency colour** — matching the discipline
measured on Stardust's own paywall.

### Tab ownership
Fixed. Sky now lights **Deck**; Club, Calendar, Post Detail and Glossary light
**Club**. Previously Sky lit Club and three screens belonged to no tab.

## Not verified
The three killed agents may have made changes I have not inspected, and may have
left work half-applied on screens I only saw at thumbnail scale. Anything in
their sets should be reviewed against feedback rather than assumed finished.
