# Benchmarks beyond Stardust — the astrology category

Stardust is a period tracker. It is the right benchmark for *colour and glow*,
and the wrong one for *what an astrology app owes its reader*. Pulled the
category leaders via Appllama `search_apps`, sorted by revenue.

| App | Revenue/mo | Ratings | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Co–Star** | $400K | **205,621** | 13-screen Birth Chart flow. The reference for Guidance. |
| Astra — Life Advice | $400K | 38,638 | Compatibility-first, only 22 screens total. |
| Nebula | $300K | 170,650 | 30-screen onboarding. |
| Moonly | $100K | 31,389 | 15 astrology screens, lunar calendar. |
| Astroscope | $10K | 692 | Has a "Big Three" flow — same content as our G-23..G-26. |

Co–Star has more than five times the ratings of any other app in the category.
Where it and Stardust disagree, Co–Star wins on *substance* and Stardust wins
on *atmosphere*.

## What Co–Star does with "your chart"

Thirteen screens: a **table** of every placement, a **wheel**, then **one
screen per placement** — Sun, Mars, Ascendant, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter,
Saturn, Uranus — swiped through with pagination dots.

```
Pill brand header
Segmented top tabs      Chart | Saved | Settings
Pagination dots
┌─ hairline card ──────────────────────────────┐
│  Sun in Scorpio            serif heading     │
│  Two full paragraphs of specific copy.       │
│  ⟨⟩ Add friends to see who's similar         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
      monochrome surreal art, unframed
┌─ next card ...
Bottom tab nav
```

### Six things worth taking

1. **The reading is the hero.** Two real paragraphs, specific to the placement
   and the house. Nothing decorative competes with the text.
2. **Hairline cards, never filled.** A 1px warm-grey rule. Our clone screens
   fill every card with a tinted plate, which is heavier than it needs to be.
3. **Art sits *between* cards, unframed** — a breath between readings rather
   than an ornament inside one.
4. **A social hook lives inside the reading.** "Add friends to see who's
   similar." Align is a dating app; the equivalent — *who in your deck shares
   this placement* — is stronger for us than it is for them, and it turns a
   solo read into a reason to open the deck.
5. **One placement per screen, swiped.** Our G-23 uses three tiles that each
   lead to a dead end you must back out of. A paginated series is better and
   is what the category leader ships.
6. **The chart is real.** Co–Star draws an actual wheel with aspect lines and
   planet glyphs. Credibility comes from the astrology being correct, which is
   the same reason the Virgo/AIR error mattered.

Its Big Three line — `☉ Scorpio  ☽ Capricorn  ↑ Sagittarius` — is the tightest
compression of a chart identity in the category.

### What NOT to take

Co–Star is **near-monochrome**: `#FFFFFF` canvas, `#111111` text, `#D8D5CF`
rules, and one tiny `#D14B3F` accent mark. Its personality is typography and
surreal collage, not colour. Align has deliberately chosen the aura as its
instrument, so we keep the dark canvas and the twelve hues — but it is worth
knowing that the most-loved app in this category spends almost no colour at
all, and earns its character from type and art instead.

The lesson is not "use less colour". It is **colour must carry meaning** —
which is the same conclusion the Stardust measurements reached from the other
direction.
