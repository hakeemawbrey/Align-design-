Pisces falls for who you could be. Show them who you are, early.

Pisces is mutable water, ruled by Jupiter and, in modern astrology, Neptune. Dreamy, intuitive, romantic.

They see the best in people. Sometimes the best that is not quite there yet. They fall for the sketch and fill in the colours themselves, beautifully and sometimes inaccurately.

That makes them generous. It also makes them vulnerable to loving potential instead of reality.

If you love a Pisces, be real with them. Show them the true you early. They will love that more than any fantasy.
