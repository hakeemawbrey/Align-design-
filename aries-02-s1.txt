An Aries will fight for you in public and with you in private. Both mean they care.

Mars rules Aries, and Mars does not do indifference.

If someone is unkind to you, an Aries will step in without being asked. And if something is wrong between you, they will raise it, loudly, on the spot. Either way you never have to wonder whether they are paying attention. They are fully here.

Both come from the same place. They are engaged. They are in it.

The Aries to worry about is the quiet one. When they stop arguing, they have stopped hoping.
