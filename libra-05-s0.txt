Libra doesn't want a partner. They want a partnership. There's a difference.

Many signs want someone to love. Libra wants someone to build with.

Equal effort. Shared decisions. Two people on the same team, choosing each other. Not a fan, not a project, not a rescuer. An equal who shows up with half the plan.

Libra rules the seventh house, the house of partnership. Working together is in their nature.

If you want a Libra, do not just admire them. Collaborate with them. Show them you are in it as equals.
