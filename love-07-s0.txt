You can't text someone into loving you. Put the phone down.

You have drafted it four times. Funny version, casual version, honest version, the one you will delete.

Here is the thing about the perfect message. It does not exist, because the problem was never the wording.

If someone wants to talk to you, a plain hello works. If they do not, no sentence will change that.

Your energy is better spent somewhere it is received. Put the phone face down. Go outside. Let the silence answer the question you have been avoiding.
