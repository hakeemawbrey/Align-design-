# Align at founder camp: the demo guide

## Before you fly

1. **Vercel:** import `hakeemawbrey/align-design-` at vercel.com/new with
   **Root Directory `app`**. Open the link on your phone, then Share →
   **Add to Home Screen**.
2. **Supabase (about 5 min):** follow "Set up Supabase" in the README, then
   add the two env vars in Vercel and redeploy. Without it the app still
   works, saving on each phone, but two people can't match each other.
3. **Practice:** run the 60-second walkthrough below three times on your
   actual phone.
4. **Charge:** a power bank. Demos die at 12%.

## The 60-second walkthrough

1. **Open Align.** "Every night you're dealt a hand of cards. No photos."
2. **Deck:** release Maya (swipe left), align Rio (swipe right).
   "It's a game: draws of five, then an event card."
3. **Juniper:** hold to peek ("three peeks a night"), then align.
   **It's mutual**, a Rare pull.
4. **The reveal:** the cards flip, and only now do you see faces.
5. **Chat:** the conversation starts from something on her card.
6. **Talk card:** tap the card button in the chat, then play "Planned date or
   spontaneous date?". Juniper's answer stays sealed until you answer yours.
   "It's how you find out the stuff that matters: kids, money, how you fight.
   And you collect the cards from packs."
7. **Place card:** in the same card sheet, open **Places** and play Agnes Cafe.
   Juniper says yes, and it's a date. Tap **Check in**: "Partner venues give you
   a card you can only get there, and they pay us for the date."
8. **The hand** (the fanned-cards button next to the sky, or in any chat) →
   **Open packs**. It's one binder everywhere: packs, signs, talk cards,
   places, events and the people you've traded. Open the **Align pack**. Tap through all 12
   cards like a real pack: talk cards, a place, events, energy, and the sign
   card last. It's numbered (№ 1,204 / 5,000). "Every season prints a fixed run, online and
   in real life. When it's gone it's gone." Show **Season** for the print runs
   and the season pass.
9. **Matches → Binder:** after three days aligned you trade copies of your cards.
   "Collectible. One day there are rare cards of famous people."
10. Hand them the phone. **You → Reset demo** (tap twice) between people.

## "Download it and match with me" (needs Supabase)

1. They open the Vercel link and tap **New here**.
2. Two minutes of onboarding: birthday, birth time, their sky. On the last step
   they type a first name and a one-line bio and add a photo.
3. Their card is dealt first in your deck (**NEW · NEAR YOU**). Align on each
   other and it's a match on both phones. Then message live.

## "What's real?"

- **Real:** the app (installable, and an iOS build), the backend (accounts,
  swipes, matches, chat, photos on Supabase, with server-side matching and
  row-level security), two real people matching and chatting, and the chart
  math (positions computed, not made up).
- **Seeded:** tonight's people, Juniper aligning back, and her replies. They
  stand in for a city's worth of users.
- **Next:** a real dealing algorithm across real users, verification and
  safety, and push notifications.

## The card, in one breath

> holds. Each one says how strong it is. Rare pull is the rarest card in the
> deck. There's no compatibility score, by design, and no photo until you
