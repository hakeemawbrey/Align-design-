# Align: how the cards make money

Align is a dating app played as a card game. The subscription pays the bills,
and the cards make Align a collectible people keep, both online and in real
life. That second part is the hedge against dating-app churn: people cancel
the app once they find someone, but they keep collecting the cards.

## Card families

| Family | Where | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Character cards** (people) | Online only, never printed | Dealt every night |
| **Sign archetypes** (12, e.g. Libra · The Diplomat) | Online + printed | Sign packs, season packs |
| **Talk cards** (51 questions, tarot-styled) | Online + printed decks | Packs, the free card of the day |
| **Event cards** (Mulligan, Second look, Comet) | Online | Dealt in the deck, played with energy |
| **Place cards** (Houston venues) | Online, partner exclusives | Play in chat to plan a date, check in for the venue card |

**The Align pack** is the everyday pack, 12 cards like a real booster:
1 sign card (always last, the "rare slot"), 6 talk cards, 2 event cards,
2 place cards and 1 energy card. Free gets one a day, Align+ three.

Archetypes come in three finishes: **Myths** (base), **Gilded** (gold foil,
about 1 in 5 packs) and **Mythic** (holo, about 1 in 25).

## Seasons and print runs

- Four seasons a year, equinox to solstice. Fall ’26 is **The Equinox**
  (Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius).
- Every card prints a fixed, numbered run per season, online and real life
  combined: **5,000** Myths, **500** Gilded, **50** Mythic.
- When a run is gone it's gone, so a season's cards only get rarer.
- You can trade any time.

Pricing, from the pricing sheet: onboarding offer **$88.88/yr** ($7.41/mo) or
**$14.44/mo**; regular **$122.22/yr** or **$23.33/mo**; 3 days free. Houston
beta: $15 once for lifetime Align+.

**Why shipping is quarterly, not monthly:** 3 packs a month costs about $8 per
subscriber a month to ship, which loses money on the yearly offer. 3 a season
is about $2.67 a month and keeps the margin.

## Energy

You earn energy by talking to matches (a message +2, answering a talk card +5,
saying yes to a date +5, checking in +15). Playing an event card costs 10. It
rewards actually dating over just swiping.

## Revenue streams beyond the subscription

1. **Sign boxes**: real blind-box packs themed by sign, sold retail and online.
2. **Talk decks**: printed decks of talk cards, also sold to couples.
3. **Venue partners**: bars and cafés pay for placement as partner places and
   host Align nights; they give out venue-exclusive cards.
4. **Events**: blind-date nights with partners; ticketed, private for Align+.
5. **Season pass**: a paid booster track for people who don't subscribe.
6. **Marketplace**: trading between users; later, a fee on sales.
